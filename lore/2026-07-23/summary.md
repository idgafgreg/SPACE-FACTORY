## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Pace intensity, not HP.** Valve’s Left 4 Dead AI Director (Mike Booth GDC / Edge Newell notes) tracks player *emotional intensity* and forces **Relax** valleys after peaks — constant combat fatigues; long quiet bores. Amplitude (difficulty) stays; frequency (pacing) changes. Map to us: throttle hive pressure / AlarmLevel after a breach peak so RecoveryBeat is systemic, not optional UI — then let heat/production rebuild the next spike. Not Isolation’s xenomorph Director (already mined 07-19); this is the **spiky intensity schedule** cousin.
2. **Split “wave fuel” from “evolution.”** Factorio wiki: spawners *absorb* pollution to muster attack groups (frequency/size); evolution rises from *total pollution produced*, even if contained. Steal the split for hive fantasy: **local heat / sticky sectors / belt throughput** feeds near-term raids; **lifetime factory debt** slowly unlocks nastier forms. Cold sealed decks dissipate “fuel” without rewriting the long arc. Keeps factory growth = haunted growth without Biofactory drift.
3. **Suggestion beats spectacle in FP.** *Iron Lung* (Eurogamer / Bloody Disgusting): welded-shut workplace, delayed grainy camera, paper map + radar beeps — dread from *not seeing*. Light steal for first-person decks: scanner lag, muffled exterior, one delayed “snapshot” of a dark sector — never demote the factory into a sub sim.
4. **Beauty/fear refraction, not only gore.** *Annihilation* score (PopMatters / Rolling Stone / Slate): organic acoustic beds, four-note motif held back for climax; Shimmer as growing “wave.” Motif: infection that *refracts* machine hum and PA into something almost beautiful before it turns wrong — pairs with metal-as-score (07-22) without copying the film.
5. **Empty now / dense then.** *Return of the Obra Dinn* (Game Developer / CVGS): present ship is vacant; past moments are crowded with labor and death. Soft lesson for empty expansion deck: dressing and shift residue imply the crew that isn’t here — deduction loneliness, not a full mystery game.
6. **Paperwork friction is mood, not the loop.** *Papers, Please* studies (Game Studies / design essays): rulebook + stamps as physical diegetic tools create job stress. Steal only shift-board / sealed-order props that sit *beside* belts — never make document-checking the core skill. Factory stays primary.
7. **Terminals + free fog before Volumetric Fog 2.** Free Cosmic Retro computer demos (emissive screens) + MirzaBeig GPU Fog Particles (Built-in) dress lonely consoles and damaged sectors. Wishlist cheap **Sci-Fi Heavy Station control units** (~€9.20, Built-in-first) if interactive knobs/panels are needed.
