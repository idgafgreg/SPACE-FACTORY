## Isolation — lone engineer of a plant sized for hundreds

- **Source:** “Isolation” — *The Runner* magazine: https://runnermag.ca/2011/01/isolation/  
- **Motifs:** Certified engineer given charge of an automated mining facility designed for ~400 people; routine becomes instinct; AI/SIM drones cook breakfast while she stares over hectares of tanks, bores, processors; walking a plant once left blister scars; atmosphere toxic outside; pickup ships land for product, not company.  
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Empty berths and oversized infrastructure as **employment design**, not accident. The job assumes a crew that never arrives. Pride in keeping processors alive while the habitat feels wrong-sized.

## Emergency Exit — forever-ship volunteer dread

- **Source:** Jarod K. Anderson — “Emergency Exit,” Daily Science Fiction: https://dailysciencefiction.com/science-fiction/space-travel/jarod-k-anderosn/emergency-exit  
- **Motifs (summary):** Volunteers board with slogans of adventure; time/hunger/aging “solved” into beige forever; emergency exit as last human gesture; child’s scream at door-hiss remembered as the only sane reaction.  
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Soft melancholy — recruitment posters vs lived shift; “adventure” copy on walls that now read as trap. Keep light; not a hyperspace plot.

## That Lonesome Sound — company substation + humming wrongness

- **Source:** Benjamin Welton — PDF: https://ripplesinspace.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/that-lonesome-sound.pdf  
- **Motifs (summary):** One-employee company listening post; cleanup framing from arriving officers; journal of a humming intelligence / suicidal compulsion; prior outpost left blood message “They’re listening to us.”  
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Distant-signal loneliness cousin (pairs Outer Wilds light touch from 07-22) — corporate one-man post, sound as antagonist. Use mood only; do not copy plot beats or gore spectacle.
