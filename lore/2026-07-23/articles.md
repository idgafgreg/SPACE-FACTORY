## Left 4 Dead AI Director — pace intensity, not difficulty

- **Mike Booth — The AI Systems of Left 4 Dead (Valve PDF):** https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2009/ai_systems_of_l4d_mike_booth.pdf  
  - Goal: *dramatic* pacing (peaks/valleys), inspired by Counter-Strike’s natural spiky intensity.  
  - Constant combat fatigues; long inactivity bores.  
  - Estimate each Survivor’s emotional intensity → if too high, remove major threats (**Relax**); else populate interesting threats.  
  - **Peak Fade** waits for a natural break before Relax starts so the current fight can finish.  
  - Explicit: algorithm adjusts **pacing (frequency), not difficulty (amplitude).**
- **Gabe Newell — Edge blog (archive):** https://web.archive.org/web/20090106130006/http:/www.edge-online.com/blogs/gabe-newell-writes-edge  
  - Procedural narrative from player state (damage, agitation, grouping). Take the group to the edge, then back off.
- **Game Developer — “The Discomfort Zone”:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-discomfort-zone-the-hidden-potential-of-valve-s-ai-director  
  - Director ≠ classic dynamic difficulty (Max Payne HP tweaks). Same map, different *schedule* of pressure. Player must keep options under discomfort — not unfair screw-jobs.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Soft director already in bible; this run sharpens the **Relax valley** contract. Wire AlarmLevel / hive pressure to intensity, then *force* quiet so RecoveryBeat lands. Do not copy co-op horde shooter identity.

## Factorio pollution — wave fuel vs evolution debt

- **Factorio Wiki — Pollution:** https://wiki.factorio.com/Pollution  
  - Pollution cloud triggers attack *frequency/size* via spawner absorption.  
  - Evolution rises from **pollution produced by machines every tick**, not from absorbed/spread pollution. Containment reduces raids; it does **not** freeze long-term escalation.  
  - Spawners absorb pollution to assign units to attack groups (not to spawn existence itself).  
  - Higher local pollution → faster muster into attack forces (random ~1–10 min launches).
- **Manu Games — “5 numbers” explainer:** https://blog.manugames.com/p/factorio-pollution-and-evolution-the-5-numbers-that-decide-your-endgame  
  - Practical split: cut pollution *reaching* nests to calm waves; lifetime production still pushes unit tiers.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Map to **local heat / infected chunk fuel** (near-term waves) vs **lifetime factory debt** (form ladder). Cold/sealed decks = dissipation. Reinforces “bigger factory = more haunted” without making the factory *be* the hive (anti-Biofactory).

## Annihilation — organic score, beauty/fear waves

- **PopMatters — Salisbury & Barrow interview:** https://www.popmatters.com/ben-salisbury-geoff-barrow-interview-2573141300.html  
  - Prefer acoustic/organic (guitar, waterphone, voice) over trendy synth beds; “suburbia to psychedelia.”  
  - Shimmer growth discussed as **incoming waves** — music structured in waves.
- **Rolling Stone — score interview:** https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/annihilation-geoff-barrow-and-ben-salisbury-talk-its-haunting-score-197947/  
  - Four-note motif sown early as harmonic bed; full impact reserved for climax. Balance **beauty and fear** — entranced and terrified. No “guns blazing” musical identity.
- **Slate — Salisbury on “The Alien” cue:** https://slate.com/culture/2018/02/annihilation-co-composer-ben-salisbury-explains-the-musical-cue-from-the-alien.html  
  - Shimmer led by **sound design**; synths saved for the third-act confrontation so it stands apart.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Infection stage that *refracts* machine-lung / PA into almost-beautiful wrongness before Mixer low-pass dread (07-22). Hold the ugliest motif for deep foothold. Motif only — do not paste film scenes or score.

## Papers, Please — diegetic paperwork friction (careful)

- **Game Studies — Morrissette:** https://gamestudies.org/1701/articles/morrissette

- **Medium — “Design of Oppression”:** https://medium.com/@ishikasoni50/the-design-of-oppression-in-papers-please-1b83b16a3079  
  - Friction is the point: single-screen cognitive load, rulebook juggling, megaphone-filtered speech.
- **Ontological Geek — tension / dissonance:** https://ontologicalgeek.com/generating-tension-in-papers-please-a-case-for-ludonarrative-dissonance/
