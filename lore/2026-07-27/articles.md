## Observation — play as the station (No Code)

- **Sources:**
  - [Road to the IGF: No Code's Observation](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-no-code-s-i-observation-i-) — Joel Couture / Game Developer (Gamasutra), 2020-03-19
  - ['Observation' video game shows how an AI solves problems in space](https://www.space.com/observation-space-game-artificial-intelligence) — Space.com
  - [Observation (video game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation_(video_game)) — Wikipedia (dev pipeline / Isolation alumni context)
- **Motifs:** Player is SAM, the station AI — cameras, valves, system menus; "you're not on a space station, you *are* the space station" energy (McKellan framing via coverage). Puzzles ask what a body-less systems agent can do that the human crewmate cannot. ISS-grounded failure modes (power, orbit, comms) inform early gameplay; later mystery. Audio deliberately stark/sparse + structural creaks for emptiness. Small Earth hazards magnify when hull thickness is the only life support.
- **Useful for us:** Diegetic systems literacy — machine faces and sector cams as the nervous system of the plant. Pairs with Duskers mediation (07-26) but through *owned ship systems*, not drone CLI. Isolation-alumni UI sensibility without Isolation shooter loop.
- **Reject:** Making SPACE FACTORY an AI-protagonist puzzle adventure; demoting factory layout skill.

## Tacoma — empty workplace discovery (Fullbright)

- **Sources:**
  - [Video: Level design for non-linear story discovery in Tacoma](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/video-level-design-for-non-linear-story-discovery-in-i-tacoma-i-) — Game Developer, 2020-07-17 (points to free GDC YouTube)
  - [The player-directed level design of Tacoma](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-player-directed-level-design-of-i-tacoma-i-) — Game Developer / GDC coverage
  - [Tacoma Review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/tacoma-review/1900-6416721/) — GameSpot
  - [Tacoma is a tense and beautiful sci-fi story about adversity and AI](https://www.theverge.com/2017/8/2/16083312/tacoma-review-xbox-one-pc) — The Verge
- **Motifs:** Subcontractor boards an emptied station; story via AR recordings of absent crew. Hub-and-spoke wings: communal hubs where paths naturally intersect vs deliberate private bunks/offices. Mundane personal props carry intimacy. Corporate automation politics sit in the background of labor on the station.
- **Useful for us:** Public/private berth grammar for facility-as-home dressing (07-26); lonely archaeology *beside* the factory loop. Corporate pre-built hostility already in bible — Tacoma's labor/AI tension is adjacent texture only.
- **Reject:** Walking-sim as primary skill; AR ghost-replay as required narrative system.

## Lobotomy Corporation — bad choices / combination disasters (careful anti-comp)

- **Sources:**
  - [How Lobotomy Corporation teaches the value of bad choices](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-lobotomy-corporation-teaches-the-value-of-bad-choices) — Josh Bycer, Game Developer blogs, 2021-10-25
  - [Lobotomy Corporation | Monster Management Simulation on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/568220/Lobotomy_Corporation__Monster_Management_Simulation/) — Steam store description
  - [Lobotomy Corporation review](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/lobotomy-corporation-review) — Rock Paper Shotgun
- **Motifs:** Daily energy quota extracted from contained "abnormalities"; player must *choose* what to keep — every choice is a sidegrade poison; combinations of quirks create the real disaster. Information is a resource earned by risky work. Meltdowns / breaches cascade through the facility. Bycer's "controlled number generation" framing: value of a choice depends on what else is already in the plant.
- **Useful for us:** Soft steal for expansion and throughput sidegrades — opening a hot bay or sticky line should create *interaction hazards* with existing HiveDebt/WaveFuel, not a gallery of unique pets. Aligns with "expansion is the risk" and ONI ripple (07-25).
- **Reject / anti-comp:** Monster zoo management as the loop; harvesting energy *from* the hive as the factory identity (Biofactory drift); chibi/employee-slaughter comedy; SCP pastiche creatures.

## Scorn — biomechanical world-as-character (careful)

- **Sources:**
  - [Behind the beautifully grotesque art of Scorn](https://www.pcgamer.com/behind-the-beautifully-grotesque-art-of-scorn/) — PC Gamer / Peklar interview
  - [‘You can’t escape from your own mind as you can from a monster’: Scorn’s original, unsettling body-horror](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2022/aug/05/you-cant-escape-from-your-own-mind-as-you-can-from-a-monster-scorns-original-unsettling-body-horror) — The Guardian
  - [How Scorn Turned the Art of H.R. Giger into a Nightmarish Horror Game World](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/scorn-h-r-giger-horror-game-spoilers/) — Den of Geek
  - [Scorn (video game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scorn_(video_game)) — Wikipedia (combat critiques / Space Jockey fusion note)
- **Motifs:** Environment as character; solid materials impregnated with organic growth (Space Jockey energy — tech and tissue as one being). Beautiful-wrong / alienation + loneliness themes via Giger/Beksiński references. Peklar: not weird-for-weird's-sake; anatomy morphs that still read as structure. Review consensus often: combat works *against* exploration/puzzles.
- **Useful for us:** Visual grammar for hive-on-corporate-architecture (already Flood/Carrion/bible). Lesson: do not add shooter combat that demotes factory skill — matches FP rules.
- **Reject:** Replacing industrial workplace with full biomechanical planet; copying Giger/Alien IP silhouettes wholesale; combat-forward loop.
