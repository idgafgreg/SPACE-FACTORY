## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **You are not a marine — you are the plant's nervous system (lightly).** *Observation* (No Code / IGF / Space.com): players operate as station AI SAM — camera hops, valves, response mode — so the habitat *is* the interface. Soft steal: deepen machine faces, sector cams, and diegetic system panels so factory state is readable without floating chrome; refuse "play as AI" as the genre. Distinct from Duskers (07-26): mediation is *ship systems themselves*, not a remote drone CLI. Sparse ambient + creaks sell emptiness; death is inches away through the hull — magnify small hazards.
2. **Absent crew lives in public/private geography.** *Tacoma* (Fullbright GDC / reviews): hub-and-spoke wings with deliberate private bunks vs communal hubs; AR replays of people who are gone; mundane props (shampoo, forgotten books) carry loneliness. Soft steal: treat break rooms, berths, and admin nooks as lived workplace bones under breach — archaeology texture beside the belts. Refuse walking-sim story primacy; factory layout stays the skill.
3. **Every expansion choice is a sidegrade poison (anti-comp).** *Lobotomy Corporation* (Bycer / Steam / RPS): quota energy from containment; choosing what to keep makes *combination* disasters, not single-monster fights; info is earned by costly experiments. Soft steal only: opening a new bay or hot line should add a *specific* compounding hazard that interacts with existing sticky/heat debt — not a zoo of unique monsters, not employee-slaughter management, not chibi. Reinforces "expansion is the risk" already in the bible; stay clear of Biofactory drift (hive-as-factory).
4. **Architecture impregnated, not replaced.** *Scorn* (PC Gamer / Guardian / Peklar): world-as-character where solid materials carry organic growth; beautiful-wrong before hostile; critics note combat often *works against* exploration. Soft steal: hive growth that makes corporate pipes and housings read as one organism — motif already in Flood/Carrion grammar. Refuse full Giger planet-replace of the industrial workplace, and refuse shooter combat that demotes the factory.
5. **The ghost is the HVAC.** Fiction (*The Ghost of Cerrera Orbital Station*): presence lives in air pumps, waste disposal, proximity alerts, emergency path lights — "Some of us are dead, but we keep the station going." Motif only — company systems that keep laboring for the living and the lost; no prose paste.
6. **CRT mediation + duct habitat kit.** Free CRT-Free (Built-in camera effect) for delayed/noisy sector-cam crops queued from 07-26. Wishlist Modular Industrial Air Duct System (~$15, Built-in+) so hive-as-vent habitat has modular rectangular/circular duct language if free pipe kits are not enough.
