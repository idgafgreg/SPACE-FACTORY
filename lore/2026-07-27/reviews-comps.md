# Reviews / comps — 2026-07-27

Comp notes for SPACE FACTORY. Soft steal motifs; hard refuse loops that demote factory identity.

## Observation (No Code, 2019) — systems mediation / lonely station

- **What it is:** Sci-fi thriller where you play the station AI assisting one surviving crewmate; cameras + control systems as verbs.
- **Steal:** Habitat-as-interface; sparse audio emptiness; magnified small failures; diegetic system literacy for dual-view machine faces.
- **Refuse:** AI-protagonist puzzle game as our genre; body-less play replacing factory layout skill.
- **Vs prior comps:** Distinct from Duskers (07-26 remote operator) — here mediation *is* the ship's own nervous system. Adjacent Isolation UI alumni energy without Isolation shooter.
- **Pillars touched:** Diegetic dread; lonely worker fantasy (via Emma / empty decks); workplace as trap (systems that both help and frighten).

## Tacoma (Fullbright, 2017) — empty workplace archaeology

- **What it is:** Narrative exploration of a deserted corporate station via AR recordings and lived-in props.
- **Steal:** Hub/spoke public vs private berths; mundane personal debris as loneliness; subcontractor labor framing.
- **Refuse:** Walking-sim story primacy; required AR narrative system; soft YA soap over sad/fear tone.
- **Pillars touched:** Workplace as trap; lonely worker fantasy; facility-as-home dressing (extends 07-26 Abiotic vibe).

## Lobotomy Corporation (Project Moon) — careful anti-comp

- **What it is:** Facility management extracting quota energy from contained horrors; breaches and meltdowns; combinatorial abnormality quirks.
- **Steal (narrow):** Expansion/throughput as *sidegrade poison* — choices that compound with existing hazards; info earned by costly experiments; cascade when one breach opens others (rhymes with TAB/ONI notes 07-25).
- **Refuse:** Monster-collection loop; harvesting the hive as the factory's purpose (Biofactory anti-comp); chibi tone; employee-slaughter spectacle; SCP pastiche bestiary.
- **Pillars touched:** Factory pressure = identity (quota + risk); industrial biomass only as *pressure on* the plant, never as the product line.

## Scorn (Ebb Software, 2022) — careful visual/atmosphere

- **What it is:** Biomechanical exploration/puzzle/horror with world-as-character art; mixed reception on combat.
- **Steal:** Architecture impregnated with growth; beautiful-wrong; environment carries story without cutscenes; combat-can-hurt-exploration lesson.
- **Refuse:** Full Giger world replacing industrial workplace; Alien IP silhouettes; combat-forward FP that demotes factory skill.
- **Pillars touched:** Industrial biomass / hive visual grammar; diegetic dread; isolation > chase when themes stick.
