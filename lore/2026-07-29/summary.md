## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **The warm place is the job site; the outside kills on a timer.** *Metro* (Ars / Kotaku / 4A interviews / Exodus immersion essays): stations are wretched *life* — traders, stories, ammo-as-currency — while the surface demands gas-mask filters you must *look at* (wristwatch, fogged visor, ragged breath), not a corner HUD. Soft steal: berths and break nooks stay the warm house (Returnal/Tacoma/Abiotic already); toxic/sticky sectors cost a tangible maintenance clock (filter/scrub/seal) you check in-world. Refuse Zone/Metro FPS combat identity and open-world checklist maps.
2. **Architecture that keeps growing after purpose dies is the scare.** *BLAME!* (Yokogao / Opus / Sequart / Obsidian Atelier): autonomous builders expand a megastructure with no human brief — stairs to nowhere, cables, caverns that crush scale; mood is unfathomable alone, not chase. Soft steal: empty expansion deck and runaway belt/duct growth that still *looks engineered* but serves the hive or a dead quota — visual loneliness only. Refuse Nihei action gunplay, NetSphere lore paste, and full Brutalist replace of ship industrial language.
3. **Pressure is missing the other half of the manual — not twitch.** *Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes* (GDC / Game Developer / Voices of VR): asymmetric info (see the board vs own the procedure); simple deliberate inputs so failure is decision/comms not execution; a pacing system trips players who are doing *well* (lights out, alarms). Soft steal for solo factory: procedure cards / shift manuals that don't match live machine state; when throughput is healthy, spawn a quiet systems trip (valve mismatch, false clear). **Anti-comp:** refuse co-op party bomb genre and comedy shouting.
4. **The rules you trusted become the trap.** *Inscryption* (PC Gamer / case studies / horror-as-mechanic essays): comfort of a learnable table, then the interface/rules themselves turn hostile; cabin exploration rewards reading the grimoire beside the game. Soft steal: company SOPs / quota boards that start helpful and later force bad tradeoffs (sacrifice throughput to keep a bay sealed). Refuse card-battler loop, meta fourth-wall, and tooth-gag body horror.
5. **Tools should look like labor, not magic SF — and ships need a readable up.** *Hardspace: Shipbreaker* deepen (Xbox Wire / Game Developer nobility-of-work): cassette-futurist HUD, welding/grind aesthetic, floor/ceiling spatial cues in disorienting hulls, fuel-line placement as design dilemma. Soft steal for FP: readable deck chroma/stripe for "up," worn cutter/scanner props, monochrome board UIs. Refuse zero-g flight fantasy and union-sim primary (debt trap already mined 07-21).
6. **Fiction: persistence after purpose + clean line over a harvest.** Motifs from *The Last Employee* (routines for workers who never return) and *There Has to Be More* (immaculate factory hum over something harvested below) — original copy only; skip adult/extreme body text.
