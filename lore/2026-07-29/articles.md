## Metro — stations as life, surface as timed vulnerability

- **Who needs a HUD? Metro: Last Light and the return to realism** — Ars Technica  
  https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2013/07/who-needs-a-hud-metro-last-light-and-the-return-to-realism/  
  Filters/fogged visor and a wristwatch you must *choose to look at*; ammo readout only when you commit to the gun. Atmosphere over mutant-killing as identity. Soft steal: maintenance clocks in the world, not corner meters.
- **Under the Light – Metro: Last Light (diegetic UI notes)**  
  https://userinterfacesinvideogames.wordpress.com/2014/04/15/under-the-light-metro-last-light/  
  Watch + mask condensation as narrative-supported diegesis; stealth visibility as a physical light cue on the watch.
- **How Metro Exodus Expands Horizons Without Going Open World** — GameSpot (Andrew Webster / 4A)  
  https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-metro-exodus-expands-horizons-without-going-op/1100-6461575/  
  Feeling lost / alone / fear of the unknown is intentional; sandboxes without breadcrumb checklists; still not an open-world icon farm.
- **Coming up for air: tunnel fatigue → train as home station** — PCGamesN  
  https://www.pcgamesn.com/metro-exodus/tunnels-setting  
  After two tunnel games, team wanted air — but the train becomes the *home station that travels with you*. Soft steal: warm berth/hub that still feels like employment housing, not a cozy lodge.
- **Metro: Last Light Kotaku review (station life texture)**  
  https://kotaku.com/metro-last-light-the-kotaku-review-504780293  
  Stations bustle with wretched detail; players linger for trader talk; Metro as living thing. Soft steal: ambient labor chatter / schedule boards as life, not quest NPCs.

**Refuse:** Metro as shooter primary; gas-mask survival sim replacing factory skill; Moscow/Zone tourism.

## Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes — systems pressure (**anti-comp**)

- **Finding the fun in bomb defusal** — Game Developer (GDC coverage)  
  https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-in-bomb-defusal-with-i-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-i-  
  Core = interesting communication via information asymmetry; pacing system trips players who are doing well (alarms, lights out); simple deliberate inputs so blame stays on decisions not twitch.
- **Cutting features that were “too fun”** — Allen Pestaluky  
  https://allenwp.com/blog/2023/03/01/cutting-features-that-were-too-fun-in-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes/  
  Foundational rule: promote interesting communication; cut distractions that pull experts off the voice channel.
- **Voices of VR #98 — Ben Kane**  
  https://voicesofvr.com/98-ben-kane-on-designing-keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes  
  If either side goes silent, something is wrong; granular back-and-forth beats dumping the whole board at once.

**Anti-comp for SPACE FACTORY:** we are solo factory management — steal *asymmetry + pacing trips when healthy + simple inputs*, refuse party/VR bomb genre and comedy shouting.
