## Knowing is the infection

- [*Echoes of the Core*](https://www.freeshortstories.net/2025/07/08/echoes-of-the-core/) (Free Short Stories, 2025) — salvage crew boards a silent research station; terminals smashed on purpose; a "Core" that infects by being *known* / listened to; final warning loop: do not board, do not listen.
  - Motif: information hazard as contagion; smashed panels as containment labor; company systems that keep whispering after the crew is gone.
  - Soft steal: a machine face or archive that raises sticky interest when inspected too long — beautiful-wrong before hostile; never a lore-dump skill test (Papers Please careful already).

## Abandoned beacon: stay away

- [*Warning from the Void*](https://theartifact.substack.com/p/warning-from-the-void) (David Sheley, The Artifact, 2024) — corroded deep-space station after nebula waves; lone survivor beacon: they came when we were weakest; stay away; don't lead them home.
  - Motif: habitat failure first (hull/seals), then something that uses the weakness; diegetic beacon as dread, not quest marker chrome.
  - Soft steal: sector distress beacons that warn *other* workers away from a sticky bay — pairs with free Distress Beacon prop lead (07-26).

## Skip / careful

- *The Ghosts of Epsilon-9* / similar drill-site creature pulps — too close to generic Alien mining-rig chase; motif already covered by Dead Space / Ash-in-Transit energy. Prefer knowing-infection and beacon-stay-away over chase pulp.
- YA *Plague Ship* duct-rat adventure framing — wrong age tone and adventure pacing for sad/lonely/fear workplace.
