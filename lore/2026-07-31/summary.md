## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Danger fills the air, not the corner.** *Pathologic 2* (GameTyrant / Ice-Pick framing / Game Developer mindmap): plague horror is omnipresent dread — poisonous air, infected people, open streets that still feel unsafe — not jump scares in a choke corridor. Soft steal: sticky sectors and hive interest as *weather through the plant* you cannot shoot; you prioritize which bay to save knowing some will fail. Tagline energy: you cannot save every deck. Refuse town-sim survival meters, Dostoyevsky plot primacy, and awkward fistfight identity.
2. **Calm work lures overtime; fog closes the world.** *DREDGE* (Game Developer GCAP / Noclip design doc / Black Salt): daytime is deliberately disarming so players stay out too long; night fog makes vistas claustrophobic; fog is centered on the *player*, not the camera; unsettle via the unseen and slow reveal — monsters lose impact once fully shown. Soft steal: green-line / recovery valleys that feel safe enough to over-expand; when the surge window or sticky fog rolls in, sightlines shrink around the worker. Refuse panic/sanity meters (Mothership/Bunker already forbid floating fear bars), fishing loops, and cozy-horror as genre identity.
3. **The body is leased hardware; decay is the shift clock.** *Citizen Sleeper* (NPR / Game Developer / Inverse / Dualshockers): gig-labor sci-fi where Condition decays every cycle unless you pay for Stabilizer — corporate planned obsolescence; fewer dice = fewer actions you can afford that day. Soft steal: a diegetic *worker budget* tied to sleep/med/repair labor (not a fear bar) that throttles how much factory work you can safely do before the next shore-leave beat. Refuse narrative-RPG dice UI, station social sim primary, and stabilizer as a shop minigame.
4. **Spaces that were not built for you.** *NaissanceE* (Kill Screen / GameSpot / Sequart liminal essay / Limasse Five Steam): Brutalist megastructure as chiaroscuro loneliness — scale whiplash from tin tunnels to voids with no railings; pathways tucked where wayfinding should be; beauty that alienates. Soft steal for FP empty expansion: light and shadow as the scare, geometry that serves dead logistics not human comfort (pairs with BLAME purposeless growth, but *playable* at 1.65 m). Refuse pure walking-sim / art-gallery primary and monochrome replace of ship industrial language.
5. **Joyful factory is the anti-comp.** *Satisfactory* (heise / Unreal / Coffee Stain): deliberately dropped stamina and third-person because they broke the peace and scale of walking your plant; work-as-creation without bosses shouting. Soft invert: keep FP plant-walking for scale literacy, but refuse therapeutic cozy factory — our FICSIT-like PA stays grim/cheerful-trap, not enrichment. Factory skill stays primary; mood stays sad/lonely/fear.
6. **Fiction: knowing infects; the beacon says stay away.** Motifs from *Echoes of the Core* (do-not-listen / smashed terminals / thought-infection) and *Warning from the Void* (abandoned station beacon after corrosive waves) — original copy only; no prose paste.
7. **Work lights before another corridor kit.** Free Industrial Props Kit work light + Poly Haven CC0 industrial pipe lamp for berth/desk practicals. Wishlist Industrial Lights Pack (~$15, Built-in listed) if free lamps read too earthbound construction-site.
