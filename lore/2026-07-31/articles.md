## Pathologic 2 — dread fills the air

- [Interview: PATHOLOGIC 2 Will Bring Horror Through Omnipresent Dread Instead Of Jump Scares](https://gametyrant.com/news/interview-pathologic-2-will-bring-horror-through-omnipresent-dread-instead-of-jump-scares) (GameTyrant / tinyBuild PR framing for Ice-Pick Lodge)
  - Claustrophobic corridor horror is the default; Pathologic aims for open-town dread where danger is *in the air* and in every potentially infected person — you can run, you cannot find a safe everywhere.
  - Horror is existential/oppressive more than jump scare; the plague cannot be shot; combat is awkward and already a failed negotiation.
  - Soft steal: sticky/hive pressure as ambient weather across opened decks; prioritize which bay to keep; refuse town-sim hunger loops and plot-as-primary.
- [Pathologic 2 Mindmap: a Questlog People Actually Read](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/pathologic-2-mindmap-a-questlog-people-actually-read) (Game Developer / Alphyna Golubeva)
  - Protagonist as "weaver" connecting torn social threads; mindmap questlog mirrors gathering a picture of a dying world.
  - Soft steal only as *connection literacy*: sector boards that slowly map which systems feed which sticky risks — never a narrative mindmap UI as the skill.
- Studio page: [Pathologic 2 | Ice-Pick Lodge](https://ice-pick.com/en/pathologic-2/) — "The plague isn't just a disease. You can't save everyone."

## DREDGE — unsettle with the unseen; day lures overtime

- [Turning players' worst fears against them in Dredge](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/leveraging-the-unseen-to-turn-players-worst-fears-against-them-in-dredge) (Game Developer / GCAP 2023 / Michael Bastiaens)
  - Design target: **unsettling**, not jump-scare horror; imagination invents worse things than shown monsters.
  - Day vs night = open vs closed; peaceful day vistas create false security so players stay out too long.
  - Custom fog centered on the **player**, not the camera (default Unity fog insufficient); slow-reveal threats so unknowns stay potent longer.
- [Trawling in the deep: How Black Salt Games made spooky fishing RPG, Dredge](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/trawling-in-the-deep-how-black-salt-games-made-spooky-fishing-rpg-i-dredge-i-) (Game Developer)
  - Core loop: go out → get haul → sell → upgrade → get caught at night; fishing skill checks optional/non-frustrating (anti-Stardew fiddly).
  - Soft steal: factory recovery work should not be a twitch minigame; pressure comes from staying too long on a green line.
- Background: [Dredge (video game) — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dredge_(video_game)) — day serene / night fog + panic meter (we refuse the meter; steal fog grammar only).

## Citizen Sleeper — leased body, decaying labor budget

- [How Citizen Sleeper was inspired by tabletop RPGs and gig work](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-citizen-sleeper-was-inspired-by-tabletop-rpgs-and-gig-work) (Game Developer / Gareth Damian Martin)
  - Daily dice pool = what you can offer that day; Blades-style clocks for dread/anticipation; success-at-a-cost complications.
  - Body decay + stabilizer schedule evoke chronic-condition rhythms and corporate control of the body you work with.
- [How the gig economy inspired a cyberpunk video game](https://text.npr.org/2022/10/18/1129262218/how-the-gig-economy-inspired-a-cyberpunk-video-game) (NPR)
  - Zero-hours / planned obsolescence: Condition declines every cycle; Stabilizer is the corporate leash; energy is the daily need.
- ['Citizen Sleeper' dev on how players’ bodies shape the story](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/citizen-sleeper-gareth-martin-interview) (Inverse)
  - Precarity cascade: one small wrong turn becomes a horrible cascade; banal work enacts itself on body and mind.
- [The Making Of Citizen Sleeper](https://www.dualshockers.com/citizen-sleeper-gareth-damian-martin-interview/) (Dualshockers)
  - Gig-economy body rental metaphor (e.g. renting the tool you need to earn the rent); influence from *Diaries of a Spaceport Janitor* for human-scale sci-fi labor.
- Soft steal: diegetic worker condition / rest debt that throttles how much you can safely place/repair — never dice UI or visual-novel primary.

## Satisfactory — joyful factory as anti-comp

- [Interview with "Satisfactory" developer: "We have opened the door"](https://www.heise.de/en/background/Interview-with-Satisfactory-developer-We-have-opened-the-door-9859962.html) (heise)
  - Dropped early stamina because running out of air while walking your plant felt wrong; third-person broke scale.
  - Explicit design: factory building as stress-free creation — no boss shouting; work itself as enriching.
- [How Satisfactory is combining crafting, gunplay, and vehicles…](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/developer-interviews/how-satisfactory-is-combining-crafting-gunplay-and-vehicles-into-one-ambitious-building-simulator) (Unreal / Coffee Stain)
  - Born from Factorio obsession into FP 3D factory; combat/exploration must not destroy peaceful ambience inside the plant.
- Soft invert for SPACE FACTORY: keep FP scale literacy of walking the line; refuse therapeutic cozy factory and soft FICSIT enrichment tone. Our cheerful PA stays debt-trap flat (bible), not enrichment.
