## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Keep the lights on — then the world goes wrong.** *Moons of Madness* (Xbox Wire / GamingBolt / Rock Pocket / Funcom framing): you are an engineer whose job is systems upkeep on a believable Mars habitat (NASA / *The Martian* hardware grammar) before Lovecraftian payoff; developers aimed for *unnerve and atmosphere*, not jump-scare spam; empty hallways after a scare weaponize faint diegetic sound; puzzles/exploration give recovery valleys between dread. Soft steal: cassette-real tools and malfunctioning plant systems as the first scare layer; hive/ichor as *payoff after* workplace authenticity, not replace. Refuse cosmic-insanity meter, Secret World lore paste, and FP adventure primary.
2. **Wry melancholy of a plant that nobody can cut loose.** *Disco Elysium* (Escapist / Baltic Screen Media Review Gothic reading): industrial ruins and failed enterprises sit in plain sight while people unsee them; Kafkaesque bureaucracy and analysis replace the energy to fix; fashion/tech mix eras so the place feels stuck between times; a corpse-as-metaphor for a bloated dead system no one has tools or will to resolve. Soft steal: cheerful corporate PA + overdue work orders as *stagnation texture*; Remus→Romulus retrofit already on queue pairs with "eras layered, not progress." **Careful light touch** — refuse detective RPG, political-comedy primary, and skill-check humor as identity.
3. **Anomalies are habitat hazards, not hunters.** *Into the Radius* / ITR2 (CM Games Dev Diary #5 / MIXED interview / UploadVR): anomalies stay *semi-passive* — they punish proximity and haste without chasing; design starts from player effect, then rules (narrow AoE, probe interaction, distinct from enemies); clusters reward careful movement with valuable finds; material anomalies grown from whatever the Zone touched. Soft steal: sticky footholds / heat pockets / vent blooms as *place hazards* you route belts around (pairs with Carrion habitat; distinct from STALKER Emission take-shelter surge). **Careful:** refuse VR FPS, artifact loot loop, Roadside Picnic / Zone IP paste, and co-op sequel framing.
4. **Security booths are workplace bones.** *Prey* Talos I deepen (Slickaria place-first essay / Arkane environmental storytelling notes): department maps, crew trackers, and weapons lockers live behind barred windows that once processed IDs; terminal glow is often the booth's only light; players engage the station as *place* (boltcaster through the document slot, vents, sticky notes) to use it as *level*. Soft steal: one authored checkpoint booth per major bay with diegetic map/roster light — densifies FP without chrome HUD. Refuse immersive-sim ability fantasy and Mimic paranoia as primary loop.
5. **Distant signals make loneliness feel shared.** *Outer Wilds* deepen (Game Developer Prahlow interviews / SPIN): Signalscope finds travelers each playing *part of the same song* across distance — connection without removing solitude; score stays quiet so music is a discovery reward, not wallpaper. Soft steal when audio gate opens: hold-to-listen radio / sector beacon that proves another bay is still "playing" — Firewatch intimacy without walking-sim. Refuse time-loop mystery primary and folksy campfire paste.
6. **Naive logistics choke the plant.** *InfraSpace* (Steam / GamerScout): every good moves; bottlenecks are the puzzle; road hierarchy separates through-traffic from local access. Soft steal light: belt/lane hierarchy and distribution buffers so overgrowth strangling a bay reads as *layout consequence* (Factorio pressure kin). Refuse city-builder happiness meters and traffic-sim primary.
7. **Fiction: authorize the work when the chain of command is broken.** Motif from *The Job Interview* (Carrie Vaughn, Clarkesworld Oct 2025): last competent maintenance tech cites station regs to run diagnostics while management theater and docking politics fail around her. Soft steal: diegetic work-order / override stamps as lonely agency — workplace trap that still lets the player *do the job*. Original copy only; no prose paste.
