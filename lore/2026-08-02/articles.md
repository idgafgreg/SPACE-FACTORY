## Moons of Madness — engineer workplace before cosmic horror

- **How Moons of Madness Creates Fear on Multiple Levels** — Xbox Wire (Rock Pocket / Funcom framing)  
  https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/03/26/how-moons-of-madness-creates-fear/  
  **Motif:** Player is a *technician* on a Mars research facility whose daily job is keep systems running; isolation is employment distance (rescue far away), not a locked-door cutscene. Hard-SF believability (NASA reports, *The Martian* tool grammar) grounds immersion before psychological + visceral payoff (hallucinations, ichor, fleshy aesthetics). Empty post-scare hallways weaponize faint diegetic audio (drip, creak, almost-footsteps). Comfort/empowerment valleys (puzzles, exploration, discoveries) break tension without comedy.
- **Moons of Madness Interview – Cosmic Horror** — GamingBolt / Ivan Moen (Rock Pocket CEO)  
  https://gamingbolt.com/moons-of-madness-interview-cosmic-horror  
  **Motif:** Aim was *unnerve and atmosphere rather than scares*; Lovecraft detail/atmosphere translated into environment storytelling; Mars loneliness of being early on a dead world; inspirations include *The Martian*, *The Thing*, Alien (steal industrial isolation energy — refuse IP).
- **Mixing atmosphere and tension…** — Unreal Engine spotlight (Rock Pocket: Dintino / Ludvigsen / Moen)  
  https://www.unrealengine.com/spotlights/mixing-atmosphere-and-tension-in-the-sci-fi-horror-moons-of-madness  
  **Motif:** Good horror "not rely too much on jumpscares"; atmosphere = sound + lighting iteration; gray-box vertical slices make mood hard — light/audio setup is the craft. (Page may gate; GamingBolt + Xbox Wire cover the same design stance.)

**SPACE FACTORY angle:** Factory authenticity first, hive payoff second. Diegetic tools and failing systems are the scare layer FP must survive at 1.65 m. Distinct from Isolation Director stalking and DS Intensity bricks already absorbed.

## Into the Radius — semi-passive anomalies (careful, post-STALKER)

- **ITR DEV DIARY #5 — The Anomalies** — CM Games  
  https://cm.games/stories/itr-dev-diary-5  
  **Motif:** Anomalies vs enemies: anomalies are *semi-passive* — respond automatically, do not hunt/follow; design from intended player effect first; rules — not too large/complex, narrow AoE (distance threats are enemies), probe as main interaction. Types: global (punish overconfidence on routes), cluster (careful movement, valuable artifacts), moving (routes/teleport). ITR2 pushes *material* anomalies grown from Zone-touched objects/materials.
- **Into The Radius: How the survival shooter became one of VR's biggest hits** — MIXED / Sergei Kharchenko  
  https://mixed-news.com/en/into-the-radius-interview/  
  **Motif:** Same *Roadside Picnic* root as STALKER — steal lonely scavenger + industrial ruin mood only; refuse Zone FPS and artifact economy. Sequel adds co-op — we refuse that framing.

**SPACE FACTORY angle:** Sticky footholds as place hazards you route around (Carrion habitat kin; distinct from Emission take-shelter weather). Detector/probe labor can be diegetic when audio/tools gate allows — not a shooter loop.
