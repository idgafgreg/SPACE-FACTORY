Soft steal: cassette-real tools + radio companion + bitter-but-hopeful lonely labor before hive payoff (pairs Moons keep-the-lights-on). Refuse walking-sim primary, Lem prose paste, and action-shooter late-game framing.
2. **The plant is a house you fortify at night.** *Darkwood* (Game Developer FOV essay / PlayStation Blog / Acid Wizard interviews): started as simple tower-defense (day rebuild, night protect base) and became horror when FOV cone + sound-without-sight made imagination fill blanks; generator fuel is a tangible light clock against night pressure; no jump scares by design. Soft steal: recovery valleys that are *prep labor* (board/seal/route belts) before a forecastable surge — factory stays primary, defense is the test. **Careful:** refuse forest folklore, permadeath roguelike, and true tower-defense wave grind (They Are Billions already covers expansion-risk anti-TD).
3. **Eras show through under the paint.** *Pentiment* (SHARP / RPS fonts / ROMchip layers / DiGRA regret paper): *pentimento* — earlier images visible under later work — as design metaphor for history rewritten in place; typographic voice and manuscript framing make labor/status readable in the world. Soft steal light: deepen Remus→Romulus retrofit so older deck graphics peel into prior corporate eras (Disco stagnation eras + Romulus craft). Refuse medieval murder-mystery primary and font-as-feature distraction.
4. **The OS *is* the workplace — break it on purpose.** *Hypnospace Outlaw* (MCV / Tone Glow / Software Engineering Daily): diegetic 90s OS/browser is the play space; skilled creators intentionally "broke" UI (typos, obtrusive flows) so the platform feels authentic and slightly hostile; cheerful corporate net wrapping darker labor (moderation as mall-cop job). Soft steal careful: shift boards / work-order terminals that feel *corporate-cheap* and slightly wrong — not polished chrome. Refuse nostalgia-comedy primary and full desktop-sim loop.
5. **Corporate survival tools deepen loneliness.** *Subnautica* PDA (Medium narrative/UI essays / wiki framing): Alterra-issued diegetic device; world does not pause; sterile procedural voice ("Are you sure whatever you're doing is worth it?") turns utility into alienation; menus are a held object. Soft steal light: FP toolkit / sector tablet that keeps the shift going around you (pairs Signalis empty HUD / Observation mediation). Refuse ocean survival primary and crafting checklist identity.
6. **Air, water, and grids are the wage.** *The Expanse* Belter labor (Strange Horizons extractivism essay): Belter identity is inseparable from extractive employment; stations die when supply chains fail; "the grids" (air/water/power) are political lifeblood, not background. Soft steal light: factory pressure that reads as *kept systems* — sticky/hive threat that taxes scrubbers and coolant first. Refuse show/IP paste and revolution-plot primary.
7. **Fiction: triage the slow leak, not the alarm.** Motif from *Best-Laid Plans* (David D. Levine, Clarkesworld): maintenance tech downgrades missing lab mice while slow atmosphere loss and hull accretion go half-seen; drones fail to spot the impact under junk bolted to junk. Soft steal: low-priority work orders that are the real scare — diegetic backlog as dread. Original copy only; no prose paste.
