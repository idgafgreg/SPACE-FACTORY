## The Invincible — hard-SF anxiety, atompunk tools, minimal HUD

- **Starward Industries: Creating a Story-Driven Hard Sci-Fi Game** — https://80.lv/articles/starward-industries-creating-a-story-driven-hard-sci-fi-game  
  Marek Markuszewski: immersive story-linked mechanics; atompunk visual language from Lem's world; choices and dialogue bind the player to Yasna.
- **The Invincible Interview (Escapist)** — https://www.escapistmagazine.com/the-invincible-interview-marek-markuszewski-starward-industries-gameplay/  
  No jump scares — anxiety, lostness, fear of the unknown, fear for companions; minimal HUD; full interaction animations; real 3D helmet glass with environment effects (dust/steam); radio/equipment as survival tools; bitter tone with a spark of hope.
- **Understanding The Invincible (TheXboxHub)** — https://www.thexboxhub.com/exclusive-interview-understanding-the-invincible-with-starward-industries/  
  Atompunk from 1960s illustration (Chris Foss), Galina Balashova engineering blueprints, NASA docs; planet designed beautiful *and* eerie; subtle thriller via worldbuilding, not only narrative.
- **The Invincible review (The Verge)** — https://www.theverge.com/23943258/the-invincible-review-ps5-xbox-pc  
  Rescue → discovery → hard-SF logic for weird phenomena; tension without traditional fail states for much of the run.
- **Kotaku AU / GamingTrend** — https://www.kotaku.com.au/2023/11/dont-miss-out-on-this-hard-sci-fi-gem/ · https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/the-invincible-review-not-everything-everywhere-is-for-us/

**SPACE FACTORY angle:** Soften hive first contact with *scientific wrongness* and tool authenticity before biomass spectacle. Pair with Moons engineer grammar. Refuse walking-sim replace of factory loop and Lem/IP paste.

## Darkwood — house-as-trap, FOV unknown, generator light clock

- **Culture.pl interview (Artur Kordas)** — https://culture.pl/en/article/bringing-out-the-fear-in-players-imaginations-a-chat-with-artur-kordas-co-creator-of-video-game

- **Generator (Darkwood wiki — mechanic reference)** — https://darkwood.fandom.com/wiki/Generator
