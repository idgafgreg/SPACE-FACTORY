## Cheerful company voice over a write-off

- **Start of the Insident — Another Job Well Done (Honeyfeed):** https://www.honeyfeed.fm/chapters/201155  
  - Motifs: synthetic PA that stays warm and helpful while announcing prior crew as acceptable asset write-off; debt/oxygen deductions; hazard pay framed as “net positive” on housing debt; salvage priority of biological asset over labor assets.  
  - Design use: shift-board / PA copy that is *too* cheerful when a sector goes sticky — company built the trap; hive only inherits the pipes. Keep grim, never gag (bible). Write original lines.

## Do-not-respond / mimic transmissions

- **Transmission 13 : DO NOT RESPOND (Vocal / E. hasan):** https://vocal.media/fiction/transmission-13-do-not-respond  
  - Motifs: unscheduled signal that loops into ship systems; “do not respond” warnings; salvage crew finding empty ship and a still-playing message that wants more mouths.  
  - Design use: diegetic radio/PA that reactivates after you cut it; infection that learns cadence from our announcements. Motif only — no quote paste.
- **The Last Transmission (Free Short Stories):** https://www.freeshortstories.net/2025/03/15/the-last-transmission-6/  
  - Motifs: looping distress from a vanished ship; hull veining; sweet-wrong air; “do not respond / do not come looking.”  
  - Design use: soft echo of absent-crew archaeology (Obra Dinn light 07-23) plus biomass-as-ship infection — keep our identity as *factory under parasite*, not living-mausoleum fantasy.

## Empty mainframe still waiting

- **Awaiting Input (Creepypasta):** https://www.creepypasta.com/awaiting-input/  
  - Motifs: derelict ship; sole survivor; mainframe still on with two waiting words; authorities find no hostiles aboard.  
  - Design use: terminals that remain lit and *expecting* after the crew is gone — workplace bones the hive inherits. Do not lift plot or prose.
