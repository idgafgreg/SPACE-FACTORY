## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Power is a clock the player can feel.** *Amnesia: The Bunker* (Frictional interviews / AI and Games): the generator replaces sanity meters with fuel that burns while you hide or explore; lights-on deters the wall-crawler but flicker means it is near; noise (crank torch, tools, gunfire) pulls interest even across the map. Soft steal: sector power / HVAC as a *tangible* calm resource the hive prefers darkness and loud decks for — not a floating fear bar, and not a shooter light-combat loop.
2. **Layered systems beat one hard obstacle.** *Oxygen Not Included* (Game Developer / Klei): no need is that hard alone; intensity comes from overlapping persistent loops (air, heat, pipes, stress) where solving one problem ripples into another hours later. Soft steal for cascading habitat failure — sticky filters → wrong slurry → heat → louder machines → louder raids — without becoming a colony sim.
3. **Expansion is the risk; one breach can cascade.** *They Are Billions* (Numantian / IndieDB / guides): every step outward can be lethal; infected react to sound/activity; a single breach can turn workers inside a building and snowball. Walls *buy time*, not immortality; defense-in-depth and chokepoints matter. Steal the *expansion-risk* and cascade grammar — refuse RTS tower-defense identity and zombie tropes.
4. **Growth debt, carefully.** *Against the Storm* Hostility: settlement size, opened glades, and years raise pressure that bites hardest during storms; Impatience is a loss timer that also slightly lowers Hostility (tradeoff). Light steal only: bigger factory / more opened decks already raise haunt — do not import Queen meters or fantasy forest identity.
5. **Empty HUD carries iso horror; diegesis carries the switch.** *SIGNALIS* (design essay + rose-engine press): sparse gameplay HUD lets isometric dread land; android-flavored menus stay in-world; FP puzzle beats swap cleanly because chrome does not re-anchor the camera. Soft lesson for our dual view: factory state should live on machine faces and sector tags so iso and FP share the same truth.
6. **Cheerful company voice + debt is already the trap.** Fiction texture (*Another Job Well Done* / looped “do not respond” transmissions): a bright PA announcing hazard pay while writing off the last crew; salvage protocols that prioritize the asset over labor. Motif only — original PA copy later; no prose paste.

## Suggested design experiments (optional)

- **Sector fuel / HVAC calm budget:** a diegetic power or scrubber meter that decays while the player is elsewhere; when it bottoms out, hive wall-forms get bolder in that sector (Bunker generator grammar) — recovery beat = refuel/repair labor, not a UI panic button.
- **Noise heat map:** loud processors and player tools raise a soft interest radius the hive investigates before combat forms commit (Bunker interest system + TAB sound-reactive swarm, light).
- **Cascade checklist:** after a sticky-filter breach, schedule a delayed heat or slurry side-effect so the “solved” deck can fail quietly later (ONI ripple, scoped).
- **Breach snowball soft cap:** if a foothold infects a machine, nearby workers/props in the same bay get a short convert window — punish open sprawl without RTS building-infection comedy.
- **HUD quiet in both views:** hide non-essential chrome during calm iso and FP; keep throughput readable on world objects (Signalis empty-HUD lesson).
