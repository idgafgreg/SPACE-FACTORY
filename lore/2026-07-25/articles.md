## Amnesia: The Bunker — generator as tangible fear clock

- **Amnesia: The Bunker Interview – Semi-Open World, Structure, Stalker, and More (GamingBolt / Fredrik Olsson):** https://gamingbolt.com/amnesia-the-bunker-interview-semi-open-world-structure-stalker-and-more  
  - No sanity meter: bunker generator + fuel is the light/dark interplay. Creature prefers darkness; fail the generator and exploration becomes much worse.  
  - Dynamic stalker + loud tools (revolver, grenades, noisy light) = unscripted pressure. Soft map: sector power / HVAC as the thing that *buys calm*, not a floating fear UI.
- **Amnesia: The Bunker Replaces The Sanity Meter With A Generator (DualShockers):** https://www.dualshockers.com/amnesia-the-bunker-terrifying-generator-mechanic/  
  - Fuel burns while you hide or quest; pocket watch tracks remaining light if you spend an inventory slot.  
  - Design lesson: make habitat pressure *legible in the world* (watch, lamp states, generator room) so FP and iso both read the same clock.
- **How the 'Beast' Works in Amnesia: The Bunker (AI and Games / Olsson + Lidbeck):** https://www.aiandgames.com/p/how-the-beast-works-in-amnesia-the  
  - Generator changes monster behavior (light aversion); flicker/erratic lights when the Beast is near.  
  - Noise propagation with occlusion markup; hidden *interest* system tightens around noisy players even when the creature is far — stop throwing boxes.  
  - Steal: hive interest in loud decks / player tools; wall-forms that treat vents as home (already Carrion-adjacent 07-24 — deepen noise half only).

## Oxygen Not Included — layering and closed-loop failure

- **Layering challenges in Klei's survival sim Oxygen Not Included (Game Developer):** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/layering-challenges-in-klei-s-survival-sim-i-oxygen-not-included-i-  
  - Graham Jans: obstacles are persistent (dupes never stop breathing); even a “solved” oxygen line stays a background threat if you slip.  
  - No single hard obstacle — *layering* makes decisions intense (air + heat + plumbing + stress while you chase a megaproject).  
  - Closed loop: every input needs an output; solutions ripple (tune metal → dig more → need more air → lose natural heatsinks).  
  - Soft steal for SPACE FACTORY: cascading habitat failure and delayed consequences of “fixes,” not a full gas sim or colony-sim identity.
- **Why Colony Sims Keep Killing Your First Run (Game Foundry):** https://gamefoundry.games/blog/why-colony-sims-kill-first-run  
  - ONI framed as systems that look stable while secretly doomed — physical interactions mature into disasters.  
  - Useful warning: delayed consequence is powerful; do not hide the doom so long that factory layout feedback dies.

## They Are Billions — expansion risk and cascade breach

- **New Game: They Are Billions (IndieDB / Numantian):** https://www.indiedb.com/games/they-are-billions/news/new-game-they-are-billions  
  - Infected react to sound and activity; share tension like a swarm; colony sits amid thousands already on the map.  
  - One infected inside a building turns workers and spreads — exponential cascade. Oppression = every expansion step can be lethal.  
  - Steal grammar: noise/activity draw + breach cascade. Refuse steampunk zombie RTS and tower-spam identity.
- **The making of early access hit They Are Billions (GamesBeat / VentureBeat):** https://gamesbeat.com/the-making-of-early-access-hit-they-are-billions/  
  - Arribas: infected are the design cornerstone, not a reskin of generic RTS enemies.  
  - Soft lesson: our hive should only make sense as *factory-tied* threat (already bible) — defense systems exist to protect the line, not to become the game.
- **Swarms (They Are Billions Wiki):** https://they-are-billions.fandom.com/wiki/Swarms  
  - Announcer warns of direction; terrain can split arrivals. Final swarm heads for Command Center — layered walls buy time.  
  - Light map: warn of raid vector along heat/vent paths (pairs with 07-24 path-to-source) without TAB’s city-defense loop.
