# Reviews / comps — 2026-07-25

What to steal, what to refuse. Identity lock: factory layout primary; defense disrupts; sad/lonely/fear.

## Amnesia: The Bunker (Frictional) — strong adjacent

| Steal | Refuse |
|-------|--------|
| Generator / fuel as *tangible* light clock | WW1 bunker identity; revolver-as-navigation loop |
| Noise attracts stalker; occlusion matters | Inventory tetris as the core skill |
| Flicker / darkness changes monster boldness | Pure hide-and-seek replacing factory management |
| Pocket-watch diegesis for remaining calm | Sanity meters or floating fear chrome |

**Why it fits:** Habitatforms (power, HVAC, scrubbers) already sit in our ship-body grammar. Bunker proves players fear a *burning resource* more than an abstract bar — and that loud tools have a cost. Keep the player a worker repairing the clock, not a soldier clearing the bunker.

## Oxygen Not Included (Klei) — systems adjacent

| Steal | Refuse |
|-------|--------|
| Layered persistent needs; no single “solved forever” | Full colony sim / dupe personality comedy |
| Closed-loop ripples (fix A breaks B later) | Pixel gas simulation depth |
| Background threat that returns if you neglect | Tutorial-by-death as the only teacher |

**Why it fits:** Factory under hive pressure already wants cascading vessel failure. ONI’s lesson is pacing of *systems regret*, not building a second ONI inside the ship.

## They Are Billions (Numantian) — careful swarm/expansion

| Steal | Refuse |
|-------|--------|
| Expansion risk; every outward step can be lethal | Tower-defense as primary loop |
| Sound/activity pulls swarm attention | Steampunk zombie art / RTS unit micro |
| One breach → workers infect → cascade | Walls-and-ballista fantasy |
| Walls buy *time*, not immortality | Final-swarm city-defense climax identity |

**Why it fits:** Pairs with Factorio path-to-source (07-24) and “bigger factory more haunted.” Use for layout consequences and cascade fear — never for becoming a wall-builder.

## Against the Storm (Eremite / Hooded Horse) — light / careful

| Steal | Refuse |
|-------|--------|
| Growth / opened territory raises seasonal pressure | Queen Impatience meters; fantasy forest Hostility |
| Emergency pullback cools pressure | Village Resolve / species needs loop |
| Storms as timed pressure spikes | City-builder settlement fantasy |

**Why it fits:** Only as a metaphor for expansion debt. Our storms are hive footholds and heat peaks, not weather seasons.

## SIGNALIS (rose-engine) — dual-view / diegesis deepen

| Steal | Refuse |
|-------|--------|
| Near-empty gameplay HUD so iso dread lands | Silent Hill puzzle-box as primary skill |
| Android-flavored menus that still feel in-world | Ambiguous dream plot as required story spine |
| Clean FP swaps when chrome stays quiet | Retro tank-controls nostalgia for its own sake |
| Radio/static as infection tell | Copying unique Replika / AEON iconography |

**Why it fits:** Human decision locks dual iso+FP. Signalis shows iso horror works when the world (not the HUD) carries state — exactly our “factory readable in FP” test.
