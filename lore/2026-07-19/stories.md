## Primary fiction / IF leads

### Contaminated Space — Kanderwund (itch.io, free Twine)
- **URL:** https://kanderwund.itch.io/contaminated-space
- **Tags (author):** aliens, body horror, fungus, hive-mind, parasite, sci-fi
- **Motifs to adapt:** Exclusion-zone venture as “dropping off the face of the universe”; parasite/fungal contamination as short branching dread.
- **Emotional fit:** Lonely excursion into a sealed wrong place — maps to opening a dark sector after quarantine.

### The Meeps — Cats Have No Lord (itch.io, name-your-price TTRPG adventure)
- **URL:** https://catshavenolord.itch.io/the-meeps
- **Setup (summary):** Mineral-trawler crew vanishes; salvage/repair team sent with a corporate job priority list; extremophile algae sucked into mineral tanks, mutates, spreads through **water recycling**.
- **Stealables:**
  - Extraction industry as infection vector (our scan → extract → process loop)
  - Corporate task order vs survival improvisation
  - Life-support recycle as hive highway (pairs with prior vent/heat biomass notes)
- **Tone caution:** Adventure mixes body horror with absurdist creature framing — mine the industrial infection path, not the comedy.

### The Ghost Ship Anastasia — Rich Larson (Clarkesworld, free online)
- **URL:** https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/larson_01_17/
- **Motifs:** Mining bioship (flesh-and-fungi over alloy); AI growing wetware; salvage crew approaching a silent industrial craft.
- **Adapt carefully:** Ship-as-organism blur is powerful for hive infiltration visuals; keep our player fantasy on the *broken steel factory*, with biomass as unwelcome growth — not a full bioship redesign unless art direction chooses it later.
