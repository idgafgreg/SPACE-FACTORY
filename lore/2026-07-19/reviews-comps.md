## Newer factory-adjacent comps

### Substructure (Dubious Design / Hooded Horse) — Coming soon
- **Steam:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/3012600/Substructure/
- **Coverage:** https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/hooded-horse-reveal-substructure-a-factory-sim-from-a-top-factorio-modder-in-which-you-excavate-the-layers-of-a-rogue-planet
- **Pitch:** Factorio-style automation on a rogue planet; dig through layers; production lines can sink; underground mysteries “strike back.”
- **Take for us:** Descent-as-pressure is their vertical hook; ours is **horizontal ship logistics under hive pressure**. Watch how they telegraph “the ground is hostile” — we can mirror with “the hull / vents are hostile” without copying dig loops.

### Biofactory (Bioreactor Studios)
- **Steam:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/3469420/Biofactory/
- **Pitch:** Player *is* a rogue hive-mind brain building organ factories (intestinal conveyors, heart coolant). Grimdark biopunk; “the factory must grow.”
- **Take for us:** Strong **anti-comp**. They make flesh the player’s tools; we keep steel/salvage as player tools and flesh as the *invader* corrupting those tools. Useful visual contrast notes only — do not drift into playing as the biomass.

### Textorio (Early Access ~June 2026)
- **Site:** https://textorio.world/
- **Pitch:** ASCII Factorio-like with alien wildlife attacking the factory; deeper hostile layers later.
- **Take:** Another reminder the market rewards factory + hostile ecology. Our differentiator remains horror tone (sad/lonely/fear) and ship-as-trap framing, not ASCII novelty.

### Apocalypse Factory
- **Steam:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/2091150/Apocalypse_Factory/
- **Pitch:** Automate while defending against zombie hordes; escape-ark goal.
- **Take:** Defense-heavy Factorio cousin — cautionary. If waves feel like tower defense, we’ve drifted from the brief.

## Positioning note

SPACE FACTORY stays in the thin band: **factory layout primary**, horror annotations + hive pressure that disrupt belts/power/heat. Use Isolation/Dead Space for *pacing and mood systems*; Substructure/Biofactory as market awareness, not tone templates.
