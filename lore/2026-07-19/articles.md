## Must-read (horror systems / industrial authenticity)

### Diving into the nightmarish new systems of the Dead Space remake — Game Developer
- **URL:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/diving-into-the-nightmarish-new-systems-of-the-dead-space-remake
- **Why it matters:** Motive’s **Intensity Director** grades scare “bricks” (1–11): full necromorph packs, falling fixtures, lighting shifts, distant sounds. Areas use intensity *curves* (roller coaster), not a flat terror value. 3D audio through connected rooms foreshadows and makes the ship a haunted house.
- **Stealables:**
  - Library of low-cost dread events (light fail, metal groan, wrong-room audio) separate from combat waves
  - Tag factory sectors with intensity curves: high after a breach, low during rebuild
  - Connected ship fantasy: even if our layout is sector-gated, audio should imply adjacent decks still exist

### The Perfect Organism: The AI of Alien: Isolation — Game Developer / AI and Games
- **URL:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-perfect-organism-the-ai-of-alien-isolation
- **Why it matters:** Summarizes Andy Bray’s approach: constant max fear burns players out. Dual systems — a **Director** that knows player state + an alien that *does not cheat* — create “psychopathic serendipity.” Menace gauge rises with proximity; when too high, the alien is sent to vents / elsewhere.
- **Stealables:**
  - Hive presence that *leaves* (vents, outer hull scrape) so factory work can resume
  - Threat should feel smart without teleport-cheating onto the belt line
  - Quiet after a near-miss is a design feature, not downtime bug

### “It needs to feel like a roller coaster of trauma” — GamesIndustry.biz (Still Wakes the Deep)
- **URL:** https://www.gamesindustry.biz/it-needs-to-feel-like-a-roller-coaster-of-trauma-how-the-chinese-room-crafted-still-wakes-the-deep
- **Why it matters:** Workplace horror built on empathy + paced trauma. Rig becomes a character; a **horror clock** syncs creature pulse, weather, and environment toward the end. Recovery moments (e.g. warming hands) keep the player human. Transformed crew are not meant to be “cleared” like trash mobs — fear and grief coexist.
- **Stealables:**
  - Shift/horror clock that modulates ambience as waves approach
  - Build empathy for the ship / absent crew before biomass corrupts systems
  - Light-and-shade pacing after high-intensity sections (climb → claustrophobic interior)

### Still Wakes the Deep’s authentic horror — Rock Paper Shotgun
- **URL:** https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/still-wakes-the-deeps-authentic-horror-aims-to-channel-ken-loach-by-way-of-stanley-kubrick
- **Why it matters:** Archival research + conversations with former platform workers; Ken Loach–style human texture before Kubrick dread. Machines get space; people don’t — isolation is architectural.
- **Stealables:** Prefer authentic labor-ship logic over “horror maze” geometry (echoes SENARA / Self-Contained notes from 2026-07-17).
