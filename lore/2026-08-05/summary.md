Soft steal light: shift boards / breach reports that commit when filed — paperwork as factory pressure, not adventure plot. Refuse detective VN primary and anti-corpo sermon as the whole tone.
2. **Horror as a seriousness frame, not a chase genre.** *SOMA* deepen (Frictional pillars / GDC / Game Developer): five pillars — everything is story, take the world seriously, player in charge, trust the player, thematics emerge through play; Grip notes horror arrived because the subject demanded it, not because monsters chase. Soft steal: environment details must answer *why a worker left this*, and dread should outlast jump beats (pairs Invincible anxiety / Moons unnerve). Refuse walking-sim replace of the factory loop and consciousness-plot primary.
3. **Ships must have problems; infection hides until late.** *Barotrauma* deepen (Making Of / electricity design / husk wiki / sub design discussion): intentional cascading failure space (power/load spikes short junctions; player mistakes and sabotage as interesting enemies); husk infection can stay unnoticed — bots ignore it until vitality dips; late-stage "benefits" (pressure immunity) are a false bargain. Soft steal: sticky footholds that tax scrubbers before they read as combat; factory systems that fail *interestingly* when overloaded. Refuse co-op clown energy and full submarine sim.
4. **Diegetic investigation is a held tool, not a quest log.** *Paradise Killer* (IGN / Finger Guns / Nintendo Life): Starlight laptop as repository for clues, suspects, and case build; trial when *you* decide; open-ended conviction. Soft steal light: FP sector tablet / shift dossier that organizes sticky-bay evidence without chrome HUD (pairs Subnautica PDA / Observation mediation). Refuse vaporwave comedy primary and open-world murder-mystery replace.
5. **Scale loneliness under a sealed sky.** *Stray* (Verge / Vulture / Guardian): Kowloon-dense enclosed city; biological smallness vs architecture-not-for-you; warm practical light + cold screens; infection as environmental passage. Soft steal light: FP eye-height that makes belts/catwalks feel towering; lived clutter under artificial "day" (pairs BLAME / NaissanceE crush, Metro station-home). Refuse cat-protagonist / cute-cyberpunk comedy identity.
6. **Fiction: biomass that can *choose* the HVAC.** Motif from *The Transfiguration of the Gardener Irene by the Dead Planet Hipea* (Ann LeBlanc, Clarkesworld): organism eats gardener memory while weighing whether to jam airlocks, flood pipes, or dissolve hull skin; hygiene protocol vs crew who closed vents for karaoke.
