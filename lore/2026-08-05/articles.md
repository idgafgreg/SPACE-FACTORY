## Lacuna — bureaucratic noir / irreversible work sheets

- **Lacuna review — Consider it case closed (Prima Games)** — https://primagames.com/reviews/lacuna-review-case-closed-noir  
  Sci-fi noir pixel adventure; CDI agent labor; fully voiced internal monologues as the only VO; autosave-only / no save-scumming so investigative mistakes stick.
- **Lacuna Review (Jump Dash Roll)** — https://www.jumpdashroll.com/article/lacuna-review  
  Investigation "Sheets" delivered to the phone: multiple-choice work products from crime-scene + interview evidence; submit and live with the result. Splendid log UI for orgs/conversations. Lived city texture (neon, slums, megacorp towers) over open-world sprawl.
- **Lacuna — Branching A Jazzy Future (Big Boss Battle)** — https://bigbossbattle.com/lacuna-review/  
  Sheets as branching stakes vs single-outcome detective games; menu friction can break ambience — polish diegetic UI carefully. Atmosphere (music, city motion, news screens) often stronger than the plot twist.
- **Lacuna (Jim Mander / Substack)** — https://youdonthaveto.substack.com/p/lacuna  
  Narrow linear path plays to story/atmosphere/pacing strengths; workplace-adjacent walking and vista beats without inventory busywork. Notes on-the-nose anti-corpo messaging as a risk when the campaign is short.

**SPACE FACTORY angle:** Soft steal *commit-on-submit* triage docs and lived bureaucratic texture. Light only — factory stays primary. Refuse detective VN / jazz-noir comedy replace.

## SOMA — design pillars / existential dread as seriousness (deepen)

- **The Five Foundational Design Pillars Of SOMA (Frictional Games)** — https://frictionalgames.com/2013-12-the-five-foundational-design-pillars-of-soma/  
  (1) Everything is story — no pure blocker sections. (2) Take the world seriously — every detail connects; audio-log placement itself is readable. (3) Player is in charge. (4) Trust the player. (5) Thematics emerge through play, not cutscene lectures.
- **SOMAntics: The design philosophy of Frictional's SOMA (PCGamesN)** — https://www.pcgamesn.com/indie/somantics-design-philosophy-frictionals-soma  
  Example: wall scratches swapped for whiteboard marker once character tools were known — "because the game needs it" erodes belief. Shape environments from what a person would actually do.
- **How SOMA creates existential dread (Game Developer)** — https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-i-soma-i-creates-existential-dread  
  Grip: not started as "horror game"; horror became the frame so players take themes seriously. Chase fear fades; questions about self persist.
- **SOMA — Crafting Existential Dread (GDC Vault)** — https://gdcvault.com/play/1023399/SOMA-Crafting-Existential  
  Talk framing: design approaches to communicate consciousness/self themes through play (reference for structure; vault may be gated).

**SPACE FACTORY angle:** Deepen 07-21 noon SOMA pass — use pillars for *deck authenticity* and dread that outlasts hunters. Refuse consciousness-plot primary and walking-sim replace of layout skill.

## Barotrauma — cascading systems / stealth husk (deepen)

- **Barotrauma: Making Of (FakeFish / Daedalic — transcript via YouTube)** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4a1klzgmW-4  
  Failure space as feature: accidents and deliberate sabotage can out-interest AI monsters; role-specific crisis on a pressurized workplace; monsters designed gameplay-first then made "ickier."
- **Electricity Reference & Design (Undertow Games forum)** — https://undertowgames.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=5037  
  Stable voltage vs dynamic load: crews use power for immediate needs without coordinating; reactor lag + battery behavior can overshoot and short junction boxes when load drops — cascade from ordinary operations.
- **Husk Infection (Barotrauma wiki)** — https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Husk_Infection  
  Progressive parasitic affliction; late stage grants water-breathing / pressure immunity / stinger while wrecking speech and converting death into husk; husked host becomes "one of them" enough that husks stop attacking first. False bargain / stealth progression motif.
- **Husk treatment thresholds (FakeFish issue discussion)** — https://github.com/FakeFishGames/Barotrauma/issues/14615  
  Dev note: husk (and poisons) are meant to go unnoticed if bots heal too early — stealth afflictions need delayed visibility.
- **Vanilla Subs… cascading series of events (GitHub discussion)** — https://github.com/FakeFishGames/Barotrauma/discussions/4742  
  Intentional unsolved ship problems that initiate crew-wide cascades without instantly ending the run; "perfect" subs that fly themselves kill the game.

**SPACE FACTORY angle:** Deepen 07-21 morning Barotrauma pass — overload cascades + quiet foothold clocks. Refuse co-op clown / traitor-comedy primary and full sub sim.
