## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

### Morning (landed earlier)

1. **Pride in the job is the trap.** *Hardspace: Shipbreaker* (PC Gamer / Polygon) sells meditative industrial craft *inside* corporate debt and disposable labor — radio banter shut down by the company, pride in a clean cut while the ledger owns you. Our factory loop already has “build well under pressure”; dress it with leased tools, shift clocks, and empty crew berths so loneliness reads as *employment*, not a locked-door cutscene.
2. **The freighter can be hell without a monster.** *Mouthwashing* (Guardian) uses U-boat corridors that expand/contract like guts, romantic LED sunsets on broken panels, and a ship that mutates with crew psychosis. Steal the *mood* of corporate cargo + empty routine gone wrong — keep our hive physical, but let PA screens and food processors feel sinister before combat.
3. **Cascading vessel failure is factory horror.** *Barotrauma* ties threat to hull, reactor, pumps, and wiring; husk infection stages (dormant → speech loss → appendage → transform) prove parasites work best as *timed systems*, not only spawn waves. Map: early sticky filters / muffled crew VO → mid “wrong” processor buffs → late logistics-aware hive.
4. **Diegetic tools + ambient machine lungs.** *ROUTINE* (RPS + official site) doubles down on minimal UI, CAT-as-tool, foreshadow-via-docs, and HVAC that sounds like lungs in the walls. *SIGNALIS* adds melancholic retrotech labor-camp loneliness. Pair with Prey’s “mega-dungeon station that had bathrooms and sleep schedules” — functional habitat first, then violation.
5. **Vertical factory literacy needs catwalks/pipes.** Free Modular Industrial Catwalk Kit + Modular Pipeline Pack dress multi-level decks under Built-in without buying another corridor set. Paid catwalk upgrade (~$10) only if free modules stick.

### Noon supplement (this cron)

6. **Quota makes greed visible — keep it lonely.** *Lethal Company* (Unwinnable / wiki / product case study) proves the Company deadline is the real antagonist: meet scrap quota → harder quota; fail → ejected. Steal the *feeling* of production targets that punish caution, not co-op voice comedy. Soft cousin to Shipbreaker debt and StarRupture schedule dread — our version can be shift output / repair backlog on a solo ship.
7. **Isolation dread > chase dread when themes stick.** SOMA pillars (Frictional) + Grip interviews (Game Developer): take the world seriously, thematics emerge through play, monsters are secondary to existential unease. For us: empty PATHOS-like facility energy — lonely worker + failing life-support habitat — without copying consciousness-copy plot. Sad/lonely/fear lands when every pipe and bunk *means* something.
8. **Ship-as-body is a motif, not a cutscene.** *Anatomy* (Kitty Horrorshow / Game Studies / Jacob Geller) maps architecture to organs — house gains agency, becomes hungry. *Event Horizon* production design (ASC / Vulture / Film Mining 101): techno-Medieval Notre Dame→steel; lit = 2001, dark = dungeon; core as “evil heart” that *moves*. Our factory already wants Gothic rib-cage literacy (Milham 07-20); add silent organ logic (vents = lungs, processors = gut) before biomass makes it literal.
9. **Adjacent comps: Site 17 (facility ops trap) / Haze (derelict + organic corruption).** Site 17 = mandatory employment in toxic waste plant with murderous automation; Haze = derelict craft fused with alien growth + terminal diegesis. Mood only — stay factory-management primary.
10. **Audio + particles before another env buy.** Free Industrial Sci-fi Vol. II drones + Unity Particle Pack (retint dissolves/fluids as residue) unlock machine-lung ambience and early hive ooze under Built-in. Wishlist cheap Futuristic Industrial Soundscapes (~$7) and Built-in Slime VFX only if free paths stall.

## Suggested design experiments (optional)

- **Shift-end radio silence:** brief crew chatter / company ads on deck speakers that cut mid-sentence when hive pressure rises (Shipbreaker unauthorized-comms energy).
- **Husk-style infection meter (internal):** processor contamination stages that first change audio/UI copy, then throughput, then spawn behavior — cure window exists early.
- **Door/HVAC as jump-scare substitute:** motorized doors that open too loud + air handlers that “breathe” when a sector is marked infected (ROUTINE ambient lesson).
