## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Pride in the job is the trap.** *Hardspace: Shipbreaker* (PC Gamer / Polygon) sells meditative industrial craft *inside* corporate debt and disposable labor — radio banter shut down by the company, pride in a clean cut while the ledger owns you. Our factory loop already has “build well under pressure”; dress it with leased tools, shift clocks, and empty crew berths so loneliness reads as *employment*, not a locked-door cutscene.
2. **The freighter can be hell without a monster.** *Mouthwashing* (Guardian) uses U-boat corridors that expand/contract like guts, romantic LED sunsets on broken panels, and a ship that mutates with crew psychosis. Steal the *mood* of corporate cargo + empty routine gone wrong — keep our hive physical, but let PA screens and food processors feel sinister before combat.
3. **Cascading vessel failure is factory horror.** *Barotrauma* ties threat to hull, reactor, pumps, and wiring; husk infection stages (dormant → speech loss → appendage → transform) prove parasites work best as *timed systems*, not only spawn waves. Map: early sticky filters / muffled crew VO → mid “wrong” processor buffs → late logistics-aware hive.
4. **Diegetic tools + ambient machine lungs.** *ROUTINE* (RPS + official site) doubles down on minimal UI, CAT-as-tool, foreshadow-via-docs, and HVAC that sounds like lungs in the walls. *SIGNALIS* adds melancholic retrotech labor-camp loneliness. Pair with Prey’s “mega-dungeon station that had bathrooms and sleep schedules” — functional habitat first, then violation.
5. **Vertical factory literacy needs catwalks/pipes.** Free Modular Industrial Catwalk Kit + Modular Pipeline Pack dress multi-level decks under Built-in without buying another corridor set. Paid catwalk upgrade (~$10) only if free modules stick.
