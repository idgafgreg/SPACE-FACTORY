## Must-read (station habitat / infection staging / lonely retrotech)

### Designing Prey's sci-fi space station to be like a 'mega-dungeon' — Game Developer
- **URL:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-prey-i-s-sci-fi-space-station-to-be-like-a-mega-dungeon-
- **Why it matters:** Ricardo Bare on Talos I as one interconnected mega-dungeon with simulation systems — not a string of shooter arenas. Older brutalist/prison layers under corporate neo-deco; “don’t create puzzles, create challenging environments” (many solutions in a living station).
- **Stealables:**
  - Ship sectors that once had *jobs* (recycle, eat, sleep, maintain) so empty decks feel abandoned-work, not empty-level
  - Vertical/horizontal shortcuts through maintenance that the hive can also use
  - Prefer multi-path factory problems over single-keydoor loops

### Building Prey’s Interconnected World — Game Informer (archive)
- **URL:** https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/12/09/building-preys-interconnected-world.aspx
- **Why it matters:** Level architects insisted on bathrooms, sleep quarters, food/oxygen recycle logic, and shuttle schedules — “where do these people pee?” Believability makes the later alien breach land harder.
- **Stealables:** Even a top-down factory ship benefits from bunks, mess residue, recycle rooms, and shift boards that imply a missing crew.

### How the studio behind 'Prey' reimagined space history — Engadget
- **URL:** https://www.engadget.com/2017-06-05-prey-arkane-alternate-history-space-race.html
- **Why it matters:** Visual onion layers — opulent TranStar face vs cramped Kletka metal core with exposed piping and hatch vents. Environmental storytelling via notes + architecture, not only VO.
- **Stealables:** Contrasting “company show deck” vs “service guts” on our ship; hive prefers the guts.

### Signalis brings multilayered psychological sci-fi survival horror — PlayStation Blog (rose-engine)
- **URL:** https://blog.playstation.com/2022/07/07/signalis-brings-multilayered-psychological-sci-fi-survival-horror-to-ps4-october-27/
- **Why it matters:** Dev framing: melancholic retrotech, mining/labor facility, CRT bureaucracy, combat as last resort, droning soundscapes. Elster is a *technician* searching through ruins — lonely worker energy adjacent to our fantasy.
- **Stealables:** Propaganda/posters as diegetic tone; scarce ammo mindset even if our combat is wave-disruption, not RE clone; sadness as primary color.

### Husk Infection — Barotrauma Wiki
- **URL:** https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Husk_Infection
- **Why it matters:** Clear staged parasite: dormant (little effect) → mid (speech loss, pressure “benefits”) → appendage spread → death/transform. Treatment window exists; late stage is commitment.
- **Stealables:** Stage factory contamination with readable early tells and a costly late transform — not binary infected/clean.

## Noon supplement — isolation / quota / ship-as-body

### The Five Foundational Design Pillars Of SOMA — Frictional Games
- **URL:** https://frictionalgames.com/2013-12-the-five-foundational-design-pillars-of-soma/
- **Why it matters:** Explicit pillars: everything is story; take the world seriously; player is in charge; trust the player; thematics emerge through play. Environment details must make diegetic sense (whiteboard pen vs scratches) or players stop believing the habitat.
- **Stealables:**
  - Every factory prop should answer “why was this here before the hive?”
  - Prefer player-discovered failure modes over objective arrows
  - Let sad/lonely themes emerge from empty berths + wrong throughput, not cutscene lectures

### How SOMA creates existential dread — Game Developer (Thomas Grip)
- **URL:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-i-soma-i-creates-existential-dread
- **Why it matters:** Grip: monsters are fiction you leave behind; consciousness unease sticks. SOMA wasn’t meant as horror first — themes made it horrifying. Choices exist to force players to *sit with* questions, not to award morality points.
- **Stealables:** Our hive can be scary; the lasting dread is “I am alone on a ship that still expects a shift.” Avoid moral-score UI; prefer irreversible logistics costs.

### Lethal Company and the Ethos of the “Bad Job” — Unwinnable
- **URL:** https://unwinnable.com/2024/02/16/lethal-company-and-the-ethos-of-the-bad-job/
- **Why it matters:** Meeting quota only unlocks a harder quota; suffering becomes “part of the job”; corporate cheer over disposable labor. Fail-state is being fired into space.
- **Stealables:** Escalating production targets that never grant rest; company VO that congratulates while the deck empties. Keep solo — do not pivot to co-op comedy.

### Quota — Lethal Company Wiki
- **URL:** https://lethal.miraheze.org/wiki/Quota/en
- **Why it matters:** Concrete loop math: three-day deadline, rising minimum, overtime bonus for early/overshoot. Failure wipes the save.
- **Stealables:** Diegetic shift board with days-remaining + rising target; optional “overtime” buff that costs sleep/repair integrity.

### Gothic Gaming: The Ill Body and the Haunted House in Kitty Horrorshow’s Anatomy — Game Studies
- **URL:** https://gamestudies.org/2403/articles/leblanc
- **Why it matters:** House-body isomorphism; fear without jump-scare checklist; space that asserts agency. Academic but motif-rich for “structure as organism.”
- **Stealables:** Ship sectors that feel like organs before biomass grows; dread from *wrong familiarity* (familiar deck layout that no longer behaves).

### Unearthly Terrors: Event Horizon — American Cinematographer
- **URL:** https://theasc.com/article/unearthly-terrors-event-horizon/
- **Why it matters:** Anderson/Bennett: non-boxy sets; core as “evil heart” that moves like a brain; lights-on modernity vs lights-off dungeon.
- **Stealables:** Processor halls that feel hostile when power fails; one moving industrial centerpiece that sells “the ship is awake.”

### Paul WS Anderson on Event Horizon VFX (25 years) — Vulture
- **URL:** https://www.vulture.com/2022/08/paul-ws-anderson-on-the-vfx-in-event-horizon-25-years-later.html

- **Stealables:** One coherent architectural language for the factory ship (ribs/arches/catwalks) so hive growth reads as *infection of a known body*.

### The Production Design of Event Horizon — Film Mining 101
