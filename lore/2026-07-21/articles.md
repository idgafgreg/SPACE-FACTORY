## Must-read (station habitat / infection staging / lonely retrotech)

### Designing Prey's sci-fi space station to be like a 'mega-dungeon' — Game Developer
- **URL:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-prey-i-s-sci-fi-space-station-to-be-like-a-mega-dungeon-
- **Why it matters:** Ricardo Bare on Talos I as one interconnected mega-dungeon with simulation systems — not a string of shooter arenas. Older brutalist/prison layers under corporate neo-deco; “don’t create puzzles, create challenging environments” (many solutions in a living station).
- **Stealables:**
  - Ship sectors that once had *jobs* (recycle, eat, sleep, maintain) so empty decks feel abandoned-work, not empty-level
  - Vertical/horizontal shortcuts through maintenance that the hive can also use
  - Prefer multi-path factory problems over single-keydoor loops

### Building Prey’s Interconnected World — Game Informer (archive)
- **URL:** https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/12/09/building-preys-interconnected-world.aspx
- **Why it matters:** Level architects insisted on bathrooms, sleep quarters, food/oxygen recycle logic, and shuttle schedules — “where do these people pee?” Believability makes the later alien breach land harder.
- **Stealables:** Even a top-down factory ship benefits from bunks, mess residue, recycle rooms, and shift boards that imply a missing crew.

### How the studio behind 'Prey' reimagined space history — Engadget
- **URL:** https://www.engadget.com/2017-06-05-prey-arkane-alternate-history-space-race.html
- **Why it matters:** Visual onion layers — opulent TranStar face vs cramped Kletka metal core with exposed piping and hatch vents. Environmental storytelling via notes + architecture, not only VO.
- **Stealables:** Contrasting “company show deck” vs “service guts” on our ship; hive prefers the guts.

### Signalis brings multilayered psychological sci-fi survival horror — PlayStation Blog (rose-engine)
- **URL:** https://blog.playstation.com/2022/07/07/signalis-brings-multilayered-psychological-sci-fi-survival-horror-to-ps4-october-27/
- **Why it matters:** Dev framing: melancholic retrotech, mining/labor facility, CRT bureaucracy, combat as last resort, droning soundscapes. Elster is a *technician* searching through ruins — lonely worker energy adjacent to our fantasy.
- **Stealables:** Propaganda/posters as diegetic tone; scarce ammo mindset even if our combat is wave-disruption, not RE clone; sadness as primary color.

### Husk Infection — Barotrauma Wiki
- **URL:** https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Husk_Infection
- **Why it matters:** Clear staged parasite: dormant (little effect) → mid (speech loss, pressure “benefits”) → appendage spread → death/transform. Treatment window exists; late stage is commitment.
- **Stealables:** Stage factory contamination with readable early tells and a costly late transform — not binary infected/clean.
