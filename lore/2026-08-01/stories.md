## You Dream of the Hive — longed-for collective

- **Source:** C. M. Fields, *You Dream of the Hive*, [Clarkesworld Issue 208 (January 2024)](https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/fields_01_24/)
- **Secondary notes:** [Locus short-fiction roundup](https://locusmag.com/review/a-c-wise-reviews-short-fiction-from-clarkesworld/) (A.C. Wise); [Red Headed Femme issue notes](https://www.redheadedfemme.com/2024/02/review-clarkesworld-magazine-january.html)
- **Motif (summary):** A person "rescued" from a hive-like collective experiences rehabilitation as loss; the hive is remembered as warmth, belonging, and beauty-beyond-sight; the ending energy is invitation/return rather than invasion-from-outside.
- **SPACE FACTORY use:** Soft deepen of bible *beautiful-wrong before hostile* — some footholds should feel almost like rest (warm, quiet, oddly welcoming) before they tax heat/filters/throughput. Distinct from 07-31 knowing-as-infection (*Echoes of the Core*): here the danger is *wanting* the habitat. Never paste prose; never copy unique images wholesale.
