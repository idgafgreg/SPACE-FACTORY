## NORCO — industrial landscape as complicated beauty

- [The Dragons of Norco](https://hnoc.org/publishing/first-draft/dragons-norco-game-developer-and-photographer-discuss-complicated-beauty) (Historic New Orleans Collection) — Creator Yuts on refineries as labyrinths / monsters / gods; game as composite look at place, not pure dystopia cosplay.
- [NORCO and capturing the spirit of Louisiana through sci-fi storytelling](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/-i-norco-i-and-capturing-the-spirit-of-louisiana-through-sci-fi-storytelling) (Game Developer, Road to IGF) — Intention: celebrate complexity, contradiction, and beauty; sci-fi elements meant to feel *banal* (exhaustion with acceleration, social media, algorithms, AI); alienation *and* weird community potential.
- [Q&A with the creator of NORCO](https://www.wwno.org/coastal-desk/2022-04-01/q-a-with-the-creator-of-norco-a-transformative-point-and-click-game-exploring-the-south) (WWNO) — Oral-history / photo roots; field recordings folded into sound (fmAura; backyard/park captures via collaborators); post-industrial score partnership (Gewgawly I).
- [NORCO — Raw Fury](https://rawfury.com/games/norco/) — Official framing: painterly pixel art; field recordings + post-industrial electronic score; quotidian sci-fi in industrial swamp geography.

**SPACE FACTORY steal:** factory can look loved and lethal; corporate tech should feel tired/ordinary; when audio gate opens, prefer place-specific industrial beds. Refuse adventure-game primary and regional pastiche.

## Diaries of a Spaceport Janitor — anti-adventure labor

- ['Diaries of a Spaceport Janitor' Asks Players to Find the Beauty in Garbage](https://www.vice.com/en/article/find-greatness-in-garbage-with-diaries-of-a-spaceport-janitor/) (VICE) — Goal: adventure game with no adventure; systems keep you stuck at the bottom; adventure gear priced out of reach; routine becomes practiced skill; skull-as-burden that some players keep.
- [Diaries of a Spaceport Janitor is a treasure among the trash](https://www.killscreen.com/diaries-spaceport-janitor-treasure-among-trash/) (Kill Screen) — World does not center the player; shops/systems exist for others; hand-to-mouth enforced by loops, not cutscene misery.
- [Impressions: Diaries Of A Spaceport Janitor](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/diaries-of-a-spaceport-janitor) (Rock Paper Shotgun) — Grind as identity: collect, incinerate, trade junk; life of janitor rarely high adventure.

**SPACE FACTORY steal:** residue/spill cleanup as dignity labor between hive pressure; cool toys as corporate bait. Distinct from Viscera Cleanup (07-21) — less gag, more wage-trap. Refuse comedy bazaar and cozy-grind framing.

## Alien: Romulus — utilitarian patina and era bridging

- [Spaceships, ruins, and corporate cities: the menacing architecture of Alien](https://www.domusweb.it/en/news/2025/09/19/alien-design-nostromo.html) (Domus) — Renaissance as Remus/Romulus dual eras; architecture as meta-bridge; organism produced/contained by the architectural machine (motif only — no IP silhouette).

**SPACE FACTORY steal:** daily-use wear recipe; one cleaner retrofit bay; FP authored for claustrophobia + lens. Craft only — refuse xenomorph / facehugger paste and DS shooter loop.

## GTFO — fog and light as gameplay characters

- [Interview: Simon Viklund on GTFO](https://www.fandom.com/articles/1893-interview-10-chambers-collectives-simon-viklund-on) (Fandom) — Ridley Scott Alien/Blade Runner volumetrics: light into fog, dust rays, slow fans; worn dirty futurism, nothing shiny; invisible threats readable as shadows in volumetric light.
- [Q&A: How Steam co-op hit GTFO chooses depth over breadth](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-how-steam-co-op-hit-i-gtfo-i-chooses-depth-over-breadth-in-game-design) (Game Developer) — Monster ↔ environmental condition relationships; fog/darkness change encounter identity.
- [GTFO Review: A New Kind of Horror](https://www.corrosionhour.com/gtfo-review/) (Corrosion Hour) — Bespoke per-room lighting; volumetrics; claustrophobia + vertical descent; horror without jump-scare primacy.
- [The Complex — GTFO Wiki](https://gtfo.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Complex) — Mining complex → research/containment; starts understandable, becomes weirder; Alien-inspired color/lighting notes; found-sound ambience anecdote (motif only).

**SPACE FACTORY steal:** fog occlusion + per-bay light plans; industrial→wrong staging ladder. Refuse co-op horde FPS primary.
