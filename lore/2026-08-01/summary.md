## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **The plant can be beautiful and still be a trap.** *NORCO* (HNOC / Game Developer IGF / WWNO / Raw Fury): refineries read as labyrinths, monsters, gods — industrial landscape celebrated for complexity and contradiction, not flattened into generic dystopia; sci-fi is deliberately *banal* (exhaustion with acceleration, algorithms, AI) so alienation feels lived-in. Soft steal: factory decks that earn lonely pride and dread at once; corporate tech that feels tired and ordinary, not neon chrome. Field-recording authenticity (fmAura / Gewgawly I) is the audio lesson when the sound gate opens — place-specific beds over generic horror pads. Refuse point-and-click adventure primary and Louisiana/Southern Gothic paste.
2. **You are the cleanup crew for someone else's adventure.** *Diaries of a Spaceport Janitor* (VICE / Kill Screen / RPS): anti-adventure — the fantasy quest and the cool gear stay priced out of reach while the loop is incinerate trash, buy food, sleep, repeat; systems enforce stuckness without a scripted sob story; routine can become practiced skill and dignity. Soft steal: sticky residue / spill / scrap as *job* between scares (aftermath labor without Viscera Cleanup comedy); adventure-gear as corporate bait you cannot afford. Refuse colorful bazaar comedy, gender-kiosk gag copy, and "fun grind" framing.
3. **Utilitarian wear is the scare craft — not slick future.** *Alien: Romulus* production design (Dezeen / Immersive Media / Domus): heavy-lift cranes and earth-movers as ship grammar; paint→sand→grind→repaint→peel graphics for leftover adhesive; Remus (older, grimier) vs Romulus (newer, cleaner lab) as eras on one station; sets mocked until the *camera* barely fits; real power-plant fog kept as a feature. Soft steal: cassette-futurist tools that look daily-used; one bay that reads a generation newer/cleaner than the plant around it (corporate retrofit); FP corridors authored for lens clearance and claustrophobia. Steal craft only — refuse xenomorph IP, hive-set paste, and shooter framing.
4. **Fog and bespoke light are characters; the complex gets weirder as you go.** *GTFO* (Fandom Viklund / Corrosion Hour / Game Developer / wiki Complex notes): volumetrics hard-wired so players cannot "turn off the atmosphere"; monochrome palettes + per-room lighting make revisits feel new; facility starts as readable mining/industrial and bridges into stranger depths; worn retrofuturist dirt over pristine SF. Soft steal: sticky/fog sectors as *gameplay occlusion* (pairs with DREDGE player-centered fog, distinct from Pathologic plague-air weather); lighting plans per bay, not one global mood. **Careful:** refuse co-op horde shooter, flashlight-wakes-horde as primary loop, and Warden/prisoner framing.
5. **You are small under industrial violence that is not about you.** *Forever Winter* (Steam / Polygon design breakdown / early coverage): scavengers under gargantuan war machines; factions have their own agendas; you are not on either side. Soft steal for iso scale literacy only — distant machinery / hive mass that moves with its own goals while the worker keeps the line. Refuse extraction-shooter loot loops, co-op squad fantasy, and Warhammer-grimdark replace of sad/lonely tone.
6. **Fiction: the hive as longed-for home.** Motif from *You Dream of the Hive* (Clarkesworld — C. M. Fields): beautiful-wrong collective that feels like warmth and belonging; rescue reads as deprivation; the station becomes an invitation. Soft deepen of bible "beautiful-wrong before hostile" — some footholds should tempt (quiet, warm, almost peaceful) before they cost a bay. Original copy only; no prose paste.
7. **Diegetic power props before another light pack.** Free Abandoned Factory (Lite) power boxes + Free Sockets and Switches + Poly Haven industrial wall lamp / caged sconce (pipe lamp already 07-31). Wishlist Fuse Box Prop (~$15.99, Built-in listed) for green/red/off fault bulbs on a lived breaker panel.
