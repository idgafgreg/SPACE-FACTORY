## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Art-direct dread through interrogation lighting and diegetic wayfinding.** Ian Milham’s Dead Space talks (Kotaku write-up of the 2009 art session + free GDC Vault DS2 lecture) stress Fincher-hopeless color, Gothic “rib cage” ship anatomy, dentist-arm spots that feel like interrogation (little bounce light), and *no HUD* — health/doors/landmarks live in the world. Our factory ship already wants this: lamp flicker, Japanese-subway style sector tags, lived-in posters over floating UI chrome.
2. **Authenticity first, then “terrible beauty.”** Still Wakes pillars (Making it Personal / Authenticity / Terrible Beauty) and Xbox Wire inspirations (*The Thing*, *Annihilation*, Suspiria color, Southern Reach audio) say: sell the labor habitat, then let biomass be mesmerizing *and* wrong. Audio treats the metal structure as a living organism — call-and-response ambience, not isolated sting library.
3. **Hive ecology should stage: infect → specialize → coordinate.** Flood design (Halopedia / Wikipedia / RPS on infection-form silhouette) is a clear ladder: fragile infection forms seek hosts; combat forms seize space; carriers/spores spread; intelligence scales with biomass consumed; cold/hostile climate slows growth. Map to factory: early sticky residue and “wrong slurry,” mid-game vent carriers, late hive that *uses* our heat/logistics — not one monster type forever.
4. **Corporate / cycle pressure is a factory-horror cousin.** *StarRupture* (convict debt + solar Rupture wipe/rebuild) and *Drill Deep* (incremental dread as you dig) show automation games leaning into schedule dread without becoming pure shooters. Steal the *feeling* of owed quotas and escalating wrongness — keep our identity as ship factory + parasite, not open-world planet builder.
5. **Project is Built-in RP today.** Prefer Built-in-compatible free kits / audio tools (3D Free Modular Kit, EmaceArt FBX, Janky Audio) before URP-only corridor packs. Occlusion + reverb zones sell lonely decks cheaply.

## Suggested design experiments (optional)

- **Sector “dentist lights”:** few hard spots on arms/trusses, almost no fill — when hive nears, one arm dies and the room goes blacker, not flashier.
- **Infection staging tied to factory heat:** cold sealed decks stay “quiet”; processors/heat raise infection form spawn rate (Flood cold-resistance motif inverted as our soft pollution analog).
- **Diegetic radio / PA:** short ship VO through reverb-occluded speakers; when occluded wrong, player hears muffled “crew” that isn’t there.
