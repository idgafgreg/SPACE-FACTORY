# Reviews / comps — 2026-07-20

## Comp watch (new this run)

### StarRupture (Creepy Jar) — factory + corporate debt + catastrophe cycle
- **Studio rename / premise:** https://creepyjar.com/en/new-name-starrupture/
- **Early Access review:** https://www.glitchrant.com/starrupture-early-access-review/
- **Automation-angle review:** https://www.culturecombine.com/post/starrupture-review-an-explosive-twist-on-factorio-style-automation
- **What it is:** First-person factory builder on a hostile planet; player as convict paying corporate debt via quotas; periodic solar **Ruptures** torch the world and force rebuild/adaptation. Survival + automation hybrid (Satisfactory cousins + environmental wipe pressure).
- **Useful for us:** Schedule/debt dread and *prep windows before a known catastrophe* rhyme with our wave prep — but StarRupture is open-world planet industry. We stay ship-bound; borrow the emotional beat of “the company still wants throughput while the world burns,” not the Rupture planet loop.
- **Watch vs identity:** Do not drift into co-op survival sandbox; keep factory layout primary.

### Drill Deep — incremental dread as progression
- **URL:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/4268970/Drill_Deep/
- **What it is:** Coalpunk / Lovecraftian idle-adjacent digger: surface base grows cozy while depth gets weirder; horror scales with how far you choose to go (“you’re walking towards it”).
- **Useful for us:** Soft model for *optional deeper sectors* that feel cursed — dread from progression choice, not only combat spawns. Tone: lonely industrial, not jump-scare catalog.

### Halo Flood (design energy, not a full game rematch)
- **Sources:** Halopedia Flood page + Wikipedia Flood development notes (see `articles.md`).
- **Comp role:** Best-in-class *infection ecology* reference for staged hive intelligence and biomass-on-architecture contrast. Pair with Dead Space industrial ship body-horror already on the watchlist — Flood for ecology ladder, Dead Space for workplace ship mood.
