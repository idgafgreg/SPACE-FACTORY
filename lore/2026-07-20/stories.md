# Stories / lore texture — 2026-07-20

## Ship infestation / process contamination

### A Singular Infestation — Jack Mackenzie (Amazing Stories, free)
- **URL:** https://amazingstories.com/2025/12/a-singular-infestation-by-jack-mackenzie-free-story/
- **Motif:** Cargo-ship “worm infestation” that escalates in the narrator’s perception — mucus pools, multiplying forms growing appendages — until the twist reframes the outbreak as chemical hallucination / solvent exposure around a single escaped sample. Crew belief and panic outrun physical evidence.
- **For us:** Empathy / false-alarm hazard — PA logs or scanner ghosts that *might* be real hive residue or solvent/stress misreads. Uncertainty is the scare; not every sticky patch needs a combat spawn.

### Tales from the Service: An Infested Recycler — Cosmic Background
- **URL:** http://textfeed.cosmicbackground.com/index.php/tales-from-the-service-an-infested-recycler
- **Motif:** Life-support / recycler maintenance mystery — gray-green slime that didn’t arrive via normal inflow; xenobiome colonization of waste processors; maintenance mode as a vulnerability window.
- **For us:** Strong factory-adjacent texture: the hive (or precursor organism) nests in *recycling / filtration / slurry* loops we already use for production fantasy. Contaminated processors as narrative + systems beat.

### A Destiny Low and Winding — Ire Coburn (Soft Star Magazine)
- **URL:** https://www.softstarmagazine.com/p/a-destiny-low-and-winding
- **Motif:** Stowaway on a quarantined ship after a lab cage fails; contamination travels vents; no safe corner; characters choose connection vs self-preservation while already marked.
- **For us:** Lonely worker + sealed ship energy. Quarantine as workplace procedure, not cutscene lockdown. Optional: a “someone still in the duct” log that pulls the player off the efficient belt path (empathy as hazard — echoes 2026-07-17 experiment).
