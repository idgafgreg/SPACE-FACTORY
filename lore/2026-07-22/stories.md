## Tales from the Service: The Haunted Hauler (Cosmic Background)

- **URL:** http://textfeed.cosmicbackground.com/index.php/tales-from-the-service-the-haunted-hauler
- **Motifs:** exhausted multi-shift maintenance crew on an old supply hauler; faults that feel like the ship “fighting back”; cables/systems that bind and silence a tech.
- **Use:** workplace exhaustion + ship systems as antagonist before any alien silhouette appears; “maybe it’s just maintenance” gaslighting that flips to literal danger.
- **Avoid:** copying plot beats or dialogue; keep our hive physical and factory-tied.

## Hanna (Ryan Williamson)

- **URL:** https://www.ryanwilliamson.com/hanna/
- **Motifs:** single engineer on a remote relay station; talks to the station as “Hanna”; corporation ships a psychiatrist each resupply because solitude breaks people; hallucination-or-AI ambiguity; love for the workplace voice that may not be real.
- **Use:** lonely worker fantasy — patched tools, empty cycles, naming the ship; diegetic VO that might be recording / might be wrong.
- **Avoid:** romance subplot as game focus; copying character/plot.

## In the Quiet Night (Victor Jimenez)

- **URL:** https://serialassemblerofwords.substack.com/p/in-the-quiet-night
- **Motifs:** end-of-rotation worker on a rundown He-3 mining station; wants only to go home to kids; ignored “wrong” cargo; maintenance drones and soft singing from speakers that become invitation/horror.
- **Use:** schedule vs survival — quiet night before leave; automation that should help becomes the scare; speakers as lungs/mouth.
- **Avoid:** pasting prose; keep dread sad, not jump-scare meme.

## The Journal of the Last Crewman of the CTV Rainy Butler (blog fiction)

- **URL:** http://vmunashii.blogspot.com/2017/07/short-fiction-journal-of-last-crewman.html
- **Motifs:** lone crewman left with life support intact but engines/comms sabotaged; maintenance routine as sanity scaffold; loneliness invents companions who take over the routine.
- **Use:** ritual labor as psychological armor; empty ship that still needs daily checks.
- **Avoid:** hallucination-companion as primary narrative arc for SPACE FACTORY (atmosphere > story).
