## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Some storms you can hear coming — the job is to get inside.** *S.T.A.L.K.E.R.* Emissions (GSC support / IGN / Zone design diaries): a lethal energy surge forces everyone into closed shelter until it passes; radio/PDA warn and mark nearest cover; anomalies often need detectors, not eyes. Soft steal: a forecastable hive *surge window* that sends the worker into a sealed bay or under-deck while the plant holds — distinct from L4D soft intensity valleys (pace frequency) and Bunker spent calm (resource you burn). Refuse Zone open-world shooter, artifact loot loop, and mutant bestiary as identity.
2. **Intimate space stays first-person; the hostile world can keep its overview.** *Returnal* (Housemarque / Push Square / PS Blog / A Sound Effect): primary loop is third-person action, but house/ship sequences drop into FP for psychological intimacy; narrative is opt-in logs and mystery that *haunts* rather than jump-scares; audio is spatial survival info, not garnish. Soft steal for our dual-mode: berths and break rooms read denser at eye level while iso keeps factory literacy — Returnal's house grammar, not its bullet-hell. Refuse roguelike death-cycle as the loop and chronosickness as a story primary.
3. **The radio is the other person on shift.** *Firewatch* (Campo Santo / Verge / VentureBeat / RPS / GDC "Do You Copy?"): a lonely employment fantasy where the only relationship is a walkie; diegetic radio UI; hold-to-talk physicality; conversation that can be declined. Soft steal: deepen the bible's distant-shift-channel motif — company/crew voice that never arrives in person, intimacy through incomplete audio, never a floating chat panel. Refuse walking-sim primacy and relationship-plot as the skill.
4. **Failure taxes the worker; leave treats it — not a fear meter.** *Mothership* RPG (Tuesday Knight / Player's Survival Guide framing): failed checks accumulate Stress; Panic is a check against that debt; Shore Leave is where Conditions get treated; Teamster class is blue-collar space labor. Soft steal only as *texture*: failed containment / noisy labor raises sticky interest or next-wave debt — never a floating panic HUD (Bunker already forbids fear bars). Refuse tabletop panic-table comedy, heart-attack gag, and crew-slaughter management.
5. **The catastrophe happens around the plant; you keep the line.** *This War of Mine* (11 bit / Game Developer emotional tuning): war is something that *happens to* civilians; the skill is shelter labor, scavenging, and quiet decisions — not winning the war. Soft steal: hive pressure as siege weather around a factory that still must produce; recovery beats stay hands-on. Refuse moral-choice adventure and war-sim identity.
6. **Night shift hears the hull first.** Fiction (*Ashfall Station Chronicle: The Long Night Shift*; *Hanna* motif only): graveyard maintenance when human noise drops and structure-sounds rise; movement on the wrong side of the plating; company shrink vs engineer who named the station. Motif only — no prose paste, no adult content.
