## S.T.A.L.K.E.R. — Emissions, anomalies, Zone as labor (careful)

- **Sources:**
  - [Emission – S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl](https://support.stalker2.com/hc/en-us/articles/27929344430353-Emission) — GSC support (2024-11-14)
  - [Take Shelter From the Emission Walkthrough](https://www.ign.com/wikis/stalker-2/Take_Shelter_From_the_Emission_Walkthrough) — IGN wiki
  - [S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl Designer Diary #3](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/stalker-shadow-of-chernobyl-designer-diary-3/1100-6087171/) — GameSpot
  - [S.T.A.L.K.E.R. I.N.T.E.R.V.I.E.W.](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/stalker-interview) — Rock Paper Shotgun (Bolshakov)
  - [Anomaly](https://stalker.fandom.com/wiki/Anomaly) — STALKER Wiki (detector / bolt grammar summary)
- **Motifs:** Periodic lethal energy surge — nothing alive survives open air except some mutants; find closed shelter and wait it out. Radio/PDA warn and mark nearest cover. Anomalies often invisible until detectors or thrown probes trigger them. Zone design diaries stress emptiness, poisonous fogs, and a reconstructed industrial dead city as *workplace of scavengers*, not theme-park horror. Authenticity of Soviet industrial ruin was a primary goal.
- **Useful for us:** A forecastable *surge window* that interrupts factory labor and forces a retreat into sealed bays — distinct from L4D soft valleys (pace peak frequency) and Bunker spent calm (resource you burn). Detector-before-eyes pairs with Iron Lung / Duskers mediation. Industrial emptiness = lived Zone labor, not generic fog.
- **Reject:** Open-world shooter identity; artifact economy; mutant bestiary as the skill; radiation meter chrome.

## Returnal — dual intimacy, haunt not jump scare (careful)

- **Sources:**
  - [Interview: Returnal PS5 Developer Housemarque Talks Story, Replayability, and Value](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2021/02/interview_returnal_ps5_developer_housemarque_talks_story_replayability_and_value) — Push Square
  - [Returnal: Four mysteries from the new story trailer answered](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/03/17/returnal-four-mysteries-from-the-new-story-trailer-answered/) — PlayStation Blog
  - [Returnal: How its glorious, dark electronic sound was made](https://www.asoundeffect.com/returnal-game-audio/) — A Sound Effect (Couthier / Gumbleton)
  - [A third-person, action, roguelike, bullet-hell arcade thriller: The making of Returnal](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/a-third-person-action-roguelike-bullet-hell-arcade-thriller-the-making-of-returnal) — Game Developer / GDC 2022 coverage (Krueger)
  - [Interview with Eevi Korhonen, Narrative Designer on Returnal](https://housemarque.com/news/2021/10/12/interview-with-eevi-korhonen) — Housemarque
- **Motifs:** Gameplay and story inseparable; haunting = questions that linger, not jump scares. House/ship sequences shift to first person for intimate exploration of a mysterious past while the hostile planet stays third-person action. Opt-in narrative (audio logs, databank that deepens on repeat). Audio direction: unconventional/unsettling but believable; spatial 3D audio as survival information (above/below/behind); rooms auto-handle ambience/reverb portals. Worldbuilding over exposition.
- **Useful for us:** Dual-mode lesson — densify berths/break rooms at eye level while iso keeps factory literacy. Audio as layout intel (hive in ducts) when audio gate opens. Opt-in archaeology texture beside the belts.
- **Reject:** Roguelike death-cycle as identity; bullet-hell combat primacy; chronosickness / trauma plot as the skill being tested.

## Firewatch — lonely job, radio as the other person

- **Sources:**
  - [How the team behind Firewatch made a thrilling game about being alone](https://www.theverge.com/2016/2/10/10960366/firewatch-interview-ps4-pc) — The Verge
  - [Writing Firewatch, and capturing the beauty of being alone](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/writing-i-firewatch-i-and-capturing-the-beauty-of-being-alone) — Game Developer
  - [Firewatch crafts relationships through … radio](https://venturebeat.com/games/crafting-relationships-through-radio-in-firewatch/) — VentureBeat
  - [Firewatch's walkie-talkie friendship is what made it stand out](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/firewatchs-walkie-talkie-friendship-is-what-made-it-stand-out) — Rock Paper Shotgun
  - [How Firewatch's UI Enhances Player Immersion](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-firewatch-s-ui-enhances-player-immersion) — Game Developer
- **Motifs:** Employment fantasy of a lone lookout; beauty and aloneness as coequal design goals. Relationship exists only through walkie — casting and script lean on radio anxiety (no body language). Diegetic radio / map UI; hold-to-talk physicality; player can decline conversation volume. Isolation is the *job*, not a locked door.
- **Useful for us:** Deepen distant-shift-channel motif already in the bible — company/crew voice one bulkhead away, never a floating chat HUD. Workplace-as-trap authenticity without walking-sim skill.
- **Reject:** Narrative adventure as primary loop; relationship plot replacing factory skill; wilderness tourism tone.

## Mothership RPG — Stress, Panic, Shore Leave (light / careful)

- **Sources:**
  - [Mothership RPG](https://www.tuesdayknightgames.com/pages/mothership-rpg) — Tuesday Knight Games
  - [Mothership: Player's Survival Guide](https://www.tuesdayknightgames.com/products/mothership-players-survival-guide) — store framing of Stress & Panic / Panic Die
  - [Mothership quick reference sheet](https://monkeyslunch.com/articles/mothership-quick-reference-sheet/) — community quickref (mechanical summary only)
- **Motifs:** Failed checks accumulate Stress; Panic Checks roll against that debt; Conditions seek treatment on Shore Leave. Classes include Teamster — blue-collar space workers, mechanics, miners. Control-panel rule spreads for Wardens. Horror as mature trauma systems, not gore spectacle.
- **Useful for us:** Soft steal as *failure tax* — noisy mistakes and failed seals raise sticky interest or next-wave debt — never a floating panic HUD (pairs with Bunker "no fear bar"). Shore Leave energy maps to recovery-beat labor / bay-as-garage valleys already queued.
- **Reject:** Panic-table comedy; heart-attack gag; crew-slaughter management; tabletop dice chrome in the HUD.

## This War of Mine — catastrophe around you (adjacent)

- **Sources:**
  - [Road to the IGF: 11Bit studios' This War of Mine](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/road-to-the-igf-11bit-studios-i-this-war-of-mine-i-) — Game Developer
  - [The emotional tuning of This War of Mine](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-emotional-tuning-of-i-this-war-of-mine-i-) — Game Developer (Drozdowski / CEGC)
  - [The Making Of A Very Different Kind of War Video Game](https://kotaku.com/the-making-of-a-very-different-kind-of-war-video-game-1560735762) — Kotaku
- **Motifs:** Play civilians under siege, not soldiers; war *happens to* them. Shelter crafting, scavenging, quiet moral pauses; emotional toll can outweigh physical. Symbolic presentation lets players fill gaps. Not designed as "anti-war-game genre stunt" first — designed as an experience of civilian survival.
- **Useful for us:** Hive as siege weather *around* the plant while the factory line remains the skill. Recovery and quiet decisions between peaks. Grim, never gag — already bible.
- **Reject:** War-sim / moral-adventure as primary; multi-survivor soap; scavenging sandbox replacing layout skill.
