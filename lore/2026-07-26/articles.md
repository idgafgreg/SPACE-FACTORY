## Abiotic Factor — vibe over meter checklists; facility as home

- **Sources:**  
  - ["A lot of survival games are really boring…"](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/a-lot-of-survival-games-are-really-boring-abiotic-factor-developer-says-many-game-makers-are-too-focused-on-mechanics-and-not-enough-on-vibes) — Rock Paper Shotgun (Geoff 'Zag' Keene / Henry Feltham via AIAS Game Maker's Notebook)  
  - [Abiotic Factor (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abiotic_Factor_(video_game))  
  - [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/427410/Abiotic_Factor/)  
  - [Studio profile / narrative lightly worn](https://www.nz-code.nz/post/studio-profile-deep-field-games) — NZ Code interview with Henry Feltham
- **Useful claims:** Keene argues many survival titles ship hunger/thirst checklists without mood — unfinished ambience, no place players want to *exist*. Feltham: invert outdoor wilderness → *inside* a research facility; invert strong → *smart*. Setting is GATE Cascade, a breached underground workplace where scientists scavenge offices/labs and convert company space into a home base under anomaly pressure. Narrative worn lightly (Dark Souls / SCP energy) rather than hauled.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Authenticity-before-haunt already in bible — push further: the deck must feel like a job you sleep next to. Prefer atmosphere commits alongside systems. Anti-drift: no co-op comedy, no Half-Life meme tone, no gun-first survival. Factory stays primary; facility-as-home is mood, not base-builder sprawl.

## Duskers — operator loneliness; tech enables and obstructs

- **Sources:**  
  - [Why Duskers' typing interface is great](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/how-typing-heightens-duskers-deep-space-horror) — RPS The Mechanic (Tim Keenan)

  - [Duskers and the endless breeds of video game darkness](https://www.eurogamer.net/duskers-and-the-endless-breeds-of-video-game-darkness) — Eurogamer
- **Useful claims:** Keenan: technology should enable you *and* get in your way (Alien marine cam feeds). Command line establishes *operator* relationship — drones are cold tools, yet players bond; incomplete feeds force imagination; planning failure feels like *your* semicolon. Darkness is generative formlessness, not just black paint.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Soft steal mediation — noisy sector cameras, lagged scanner, muffling bulkheads — for FP suggestion (pairs Iron Lung 07-23). Do **not** copy typing UI or drone RTS. Lonely worker remains on the deck; tools are leased company tech that frustrate.

## RimWorld infestation — warm dark nests; hives raise heat

- **Sources:**  
  - [Infestation](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Infestation) — RimWorld Wiki  
  - [Hive](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Hive) — RimWorld Wiki
- **Useful claims:** Natural infestations need overhead mountain roof, space, proximity to colony; valid tile temperature above about −17 °C, with spawn chance falling under about −8 °C. Prefer deep, dark, far from unroofed cells. Hives can raise surrounding temperature (wiki notes up to ~38 °C) and reproduce within the room; cooling a mountain base is a fragile containment strategy that still fails under power loss.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Concrete numbers for cold-deck vs heat-processor already on the "good next angles" list — RimWorld supplies nest *preference* grammar: sticky footholds bias warm/dark utility pockets; active HVAC cooling is labor that buys spawn suppression, not a win button (solar-flare / EMI analogue = power clock from 07-25). Distinct from Factorio path-to-source (where raids *go*) and from Bunker (what player *spends*).

## Pacific Drive — garage as recovery; companion machine; distant anomaly audio

- **Sources:**  
  - [Pacific Drive UE developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/developer-interviews/pacific-drive-is-a-run-based-driving-survival-game-with-an-ever-changing-mysterious-landscape)  
  - [How Pacific Drive Pulls Off Being Unnervingly Spooky And Relaxingly Chill](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-pacific-drive-pulls-off-being-unnervingly-spooky-and-relaxingly-chill-all-at-once/1100-6523113/) — GameSpot  
  - [Unravel the gameplay loop…](https://blog.playstation.com/2023/11/30/unravel-the-gameplay-loop-of-pacific-drive-launching-on-ps5-feb-22/) — PlayStation Blog  
  - [Pacific Drive review](https://www.eurogamer.net/pacific-drive-review) — Eurogamer
- **Useful claims:** Loop = garage repair/upgrade → plan route → Zone run → return. First-person maintenance made the fantasy click. Car is sole companion; audio design softens inside the cabin and sharpens menace outside. Anomalies must be legible at driving distance — sound carries far. Garage is safe labor space; Zone is the pressure.
- **SPACE FACTORY angle:** Map garage → *your* factory bay between waves (recovery = hands-on repair, not idle timer). Map car companion carefully → pride in a clean belt under debt (lonely worker fantasy), not a vehicle sim. Distant audio telegraph for hive before silhouette — supports atmosphere bricks (07-24) without new spawn types.
