## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Atmosphere is the product; meters are scaffolding.** *Abiotic Factor* (Deep Field / RPS / AIAS notebook): survival games that only tick hunger/thirst boxes feel empty; ship mood, wind, and a place people want to *exist in* first. Soft steal: dress the plant as a lived workplace before stacking another system meter. Invert outdoor wilderness survival into *indoor facility as home* — offices, break rooms, and utility closets become the lonely berth under breach. Prefer smart play over brute force. Refuse Half-Life comedy and co-op clown energy; keep sad/lonely/fear.
2. **Technology should help and get in the way.** *Duskers* (RPS Mechanic / WIRED / Eurogamer): command-line + glitchy drone feeds make the player an *operator*, not a marine; incomplete cameras force imagination; tech is lifeline and friction. Soft steal for FP scanners, machine faces, and sector cams — lag, crop, muffle — without copying the typing UI. Pairs with Iron Lung suggestion (07-23) but through *mediation*, not sealed-tank blindness alone.
3. **Hive prefers warm dark pockets; nests warm themselves.** *RimWorld* infestation wiki: bugs spawn under mountain roof in warm-enough dark tiles; chance drops as temperature falls; hives raise local heat. Soft steal for cold-deck vs heat-processor numbers already queued: cooling a bay is containment labor, not a gag freeze-ray. Distinct from Factorio path-to-source (vector) and Bunker light clock (player-spent calm).
4. **The safe bay is where lonely labor lives.** *Pacific Drive* (UE interview / GameSpot / PS Blog): the garage is the real play between Zone runs — repair, diagnose, plan; the car is companion and shelter; anomalies advertise at distance by sound before they are seen. Soft steal: treat the factory bay like that garage — recovery beats as hands-on maintenance of *your* line — and let hive pressure telegraph aurally before silhouettes. Refuse car/road fantasy.
5. **Fog and few hunters beat swarm spectacle.** *Aliens: Dark Descent* (Tindalos / PlayStation Blog): fog-of-war hides map so imagination fills it; xenomorphs flank and retreat rather than dilute into endless trash mobs; stress is a ticking care debt. Steal *withheld map + quality pressure*; refuse squad-RTS identity, permadeath marine soap, and Alien IP silhouettes.
6. **Empty protocol keeps building after the crew is gone.** Fiction texture (*This Sepulchral Aegis* / *Lonely Ghosts*): a generation ship as machine mausoleum; an architecture drone that finishes an empty city on schedule after Last Contact. Motif only — company systems that keep humming for nobody; no prose paste.
7. **Signal and cable before another corridor kit.** Free Distress Beacon (Frontier Comms) + Polyzero procedural cables dress lonely deck signal props and hanging utility runs under Built-in. Wishlist Rotating and Flashing Light (~€18.40) for sector-alarm strobes if a tiny script + free beacon is not enough.
