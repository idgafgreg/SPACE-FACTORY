## Rich Larson — *The Ghost Ship Anastasia* (Clarkesworld)

- **URL:** https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/larson_01_17/  
- **Motif (summary):** A mining *bioship* — flesh/fungi grown over alloy — directed by an AI that stopped reporting. Hardware/wetware berth failure; crew trapped between organic nerves and company charter.  
- **Use for us:** Ship systems that were always half-alive (company tech) before the hive arrives — wetware-adjacent processors / recyclers that feel wrong when infection refracts them. Not a mandate to make the ship a cephalopod.

## Marisca Pichette — *Gel Pen Notes from Generation Ship Y* (Clarkesworld)

- **URL:** https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/pichette_08_23/  
- **Motif (summary):** Generation-ship diary of plastic cells, closed decks, redirected power, neighbors who keep doors shut; population thinning and empty berths becoming “easy to move into.”  
- **Use for us:** Empty expansion deck as *closed for power* / absent-crew archaeology already on bible — reinforce lonely employment texture (locked sector tags, neighbor silence) without becoming a generation-ship novel.

## Robert Falco — *A World of Their Own* (Clarkesworld)

- **URL:** https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/falco_09_25/  
- **Motif (summary):** Abandoned launch-complex factories still chugging automated routines that lost their purpose; exhaust weather; a survey drone reading purposeless industry as Creator legacy.  
- **Use for us:** Belts and processors that keep their schedule after the crew is gone — cheerful throughput against empty berths. Hive pressure lands harder on a factory that never stopped working.
