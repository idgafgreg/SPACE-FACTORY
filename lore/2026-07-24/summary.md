## Headline takeaways (apply to our game)

1. **Raids should route toward the loudest machine, not the player.** Factorio wiki + FFF #283: spawners spend absorbed pollution to muster attack *groups*; biters that enter a polluted chunk path toward the pollution *source* and try to destroy it. Steal the vector: hive footholds that taste local heat/throughput should path along belts, vents, and hot processors — layout and chokepoints become defense without Biofactory drift. Caps matter too: unlimited “fuel hoarding” at one nest flattens difficulty; a spendable absorption ceiling lets pressure leak to the next foothold.
2. **Study the hive from the wrong seat, then flip it back.** *Carrion* (Eurogamer / PC Gamer / Phobia interviews): biomass slips through ducts, deposits mass in wall fissures to corrupt architecture, changes size to fit the space, and makes humans look like guests in their own plant. Steal only the *habitat grammar* — vents/grates/utility crawl as preferred lanes — never the reverse-horror power fantasy. Player stays lonely worker; hive inherits the company’s pipes.
3. **Atmosphere bricks ≠ spawn bricks.** Dead Space remake Intensity Director (EA Motive / Game Developer / IGN First): graded content events include lights, fog/steam, distant audio, and falling fixtures *with or without* enemies; false-scare quiet hallways are a feature. Distinct from 07-23 L4D “pace frequency”: this is a *palette of layered diegetic events* for revisited/empty sectors so expansion decks stay tense without becoming a shooter arena.
4. **Grim without gag.** Frostpunk design notes (Game Developer): workplace sacrifice and emergency shifts tip into dark comedy if pushed too far, and read as invisible if too soft. Soft steal for shift-board / overtime copy and recovery-beat tone — sad labor pressure, not satire.
5. **Creatures that live for themselves.** Rain World AI essays (Game Developer / Vice): fauna goals are food/shelter/territory, not “be an obstacle”; layout geometry steers ecology more than scripted patrols. Light steal: hive forms that linger in warm ducts hunting residue even when the player is elsewhere — offscreen life, not aggro magnets.
6. **Security booths as workplace bones (deepen Prey lightly).** Talos I security terminals track employees and gate restricted labs with keycards/codes (Game Informer / Slickaria). Soft lesson for sector checkpoints: a dark booth with a still-lit map terminal is company habitat first — then wrong when the hive inherits it. Already adjacent to Citadel apparatus (07-22); keep light.
7. **Wear the deck before buying more corridors.** Free Yughues concrete + Real Materials samples (rusted iron) dress oil/grease/utility floors under Built-in. Wishlist Worn Industrial Surfaces Superpack (~$9, Built-in Standard PBR) only if free tiles stall. Skip HDRP-only rust-stain decal packs while the project stays Built-in.
