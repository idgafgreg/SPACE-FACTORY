# Reviews & comps — 2026-07-24

## Carrion (Phobia / Devolver) — habitat grammar, not reverse-horror loop

- **Eurogamer:** https://www.eurogamer.net/carrion-review  
- **PC Gamer:** https://www.pcgamer.com/carrion-review/  
- **IGN:** https://www.ign.com/articles/carrion-review  
- **Steal:** Biomass that *fits* the plant — ducts, grates, wall fissures as preferred infrastructure; size/shape changes to use company spaces humans treat as leftover. Humans look like guests.  
- **Avoid:** Player-as-monster power fantasy; Metroidvania ability-gate identity; gore-as-primary register. SPACE FACTORY stays lonely labor under hive pressure.

## Dead Space remake Intensity Director — false-scare palette

- **EA Inside Dead Space #4:** https://www.ea.com/news/inside-dead-space-4-the-intensity-director  
- **Game Developer systems piece:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/diving-into-the-nightmarish-new-systems-of-the-dead-space-remake  
- **Destructoid summary:** https://www.destructoid.com/dead-space-remakes-intensity-director-explained/  
- **Steal:** Graded atmosphere bricks (light/fog/audio/fixture) that can fire *without* combat; intensity curves for revisited sectors; blur scripted vs systemic.  
- **Relation to 07-23 L4D:** L4D = pace *how often* peaks arrive. DS remake = *what kinds of events* fill a peak or a fake peak. Both useful; do not merge into one “Director” feature name in backlog without splitting the jobs.  
- **Avoid:** Shooter encounter density; limb-peeling combat fantasy as identity.

## Frostpunk (11 bit) — tone cousin only
