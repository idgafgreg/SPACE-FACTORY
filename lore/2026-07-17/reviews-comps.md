# Reviews & comps — 2026-07-17

## Core comps (keep studying)

| Comp | Lesson for SPACE FACTORY |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Factorio** | Threat must tax the factory; ammo/defense is a production line. Factory stays primary. |
| **Dead Space** | Industrial mining ship as haunted house; body horror + diegetic UI immersion. Guardian piece: https://www.theguardian.com/games/2018/feb/16/dead-space-was-to-games-what-alien-was-to-movies |
| **Alien: Isolation** | Isolation + paced predator AI; ship as desertion. Early game restraint before “showing the hand.” |
| **Halo Flood / Dead Space necromorphs** | Horde ecology already in our brief — treat as swarm identity, not individual boss fights. |

## Newer / adjacent titles spotted

### ROUTINE (2025 reviews)
- Example review: https://www.zupitek.in/2025/12/routine-review-2025-sci-fi-horror-on.html
- Lunar base, retro-industrial, stealth/atmosphere over action; compared to Alien: Isolation / SOMA.
- **Take:** Atmosphere + lethal few enemies can carry dread; we need *waves* but can still keep quiet between cycles.

### Haze (Steam)
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/4034940/Haze/
- Derelict ship + organic corruption fusing with infrastructure; clinical android POV; steel/amber/sickly green palette.
- **Take:** Biomechanical merge with ship systems = visual language for hive infiltration of our factory.

### Site 17 (Steam, Oct 2026 listed)
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/3122590/Site_17/
- Toxic waste facility; survive a shift; camera/door/electrify tools; corporate dystopia.
- **Take:** “Survive the shift” framing rhymes with our cycle-based waves — factory shift as horror unit of time.

### SENARA: The Sacrament (July 2026)
- Real LiDAR ship + Unity HDRP.
- **Take:** Authenticity of industrial layout as uncanny; aspirational art direction, not MVP requirement.

## Positioning note

We sit in a thin niche: **factory-first + horror annotations**. Comps that are pure horror (ROUTINE, SENARA) inform mood; Factorio informs systems. Do not drift into combat-horror sandbox (out of scope per design brief).
