# Articles — 2026-07-17

## Must-read (horror / industrial setting)

### Why a Container Ship — Polylusion Games
- **URL:** https://polylusion.com/blog/self-contained-dev-container-ship
- **Why it matters:** Argues the trap is the job. A working vessel is already sealed, remote, and modular; horror exploits features that were always there. Maps cleanly onto our “broken factory ship” — isolation by vocation, not cutscene lockdown.
- **Stealables:**
  - ~12–15 location types recombined into a believable industrial habitat
  - Water/void as *presence* under the hull (for us: the dark outside the hull / hive void)
  - References: *Still Wakes the Deep*, *Dredge*, *Iron Lung*, early *Alien: Isolation*, Outer Wilds DLC
  - “What would actually be true on this workplace?” as a design resolver

### The Ash in Transit — Simon J. Phillips (essay on own novella)
- **URL:** https://authorsimonphillips.com/2026/05/21/the-ash-in-transit-sci-fi-horror-alien-biomass/
- **Why it matters:** Industrial hauler + alien biomass that uses heat, air, and vents. Horror begins as salvage procedure and schedule pressure, not invasion fleet.
- **Stealables:**
  - Biomass as *condition/process*, not boss monster
  - Ship becomes *usable* by the threat (our hive ship hunting / infiltrating)
  - Quarantine fear = economic/schedule cost (factory players already feel this)
  - False voices in vents — empathy as hazard
  - Witness character who notices too much and commands too little (fits lonely worker fantasy)

### Factorio product case study — Birdor Blog
- **URL:** https://blog.birdor.com/factorio-product-discipline-case-study/
- **Why it matters:** Biters matter because pollution ties threat to growth. Disconnected combat is interruption; connected combat is identity.
- **Stealables:**
  - Military production competing with science/logistics
  - Depth = interacting systems around one fantasy, not feature count
  - Default threat mode vs optional peaceful (we stay threat-on; factory remains primary)

### Fundamentals of designing a horror game — Thomas Eaves (LinkedIn)
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/fundamentals-designing-horror-game-thomas-eaves
- **Why it matters:** Isolation/helplessness as the shared trait of great horror settings; space ships as desertion engines.
- **Stealables:** Prefer unique settings that still deliver desertion (our ship factory qualifies).

### Building a horror environment (lighting/audio notes) — StraySpark
- **URL:** https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/building-horror-game-environment-ai-procedural-placement
- **Why it matters:** Practical light taxonomy for dread: practical / atmospheric / accent; contrast beats uniform darkness; localized ambient emitters.
- **Stealables for Unity:**
  - Slight green/amber industrial practicals
  - Hidden cool bounce lights
  - Accent pools that pull the eye (and the player) into danger
  - Room-scoped ambience with short attenuation radii
