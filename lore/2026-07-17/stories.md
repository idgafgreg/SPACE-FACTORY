# Stories & lore texture — 2026-07-17

## Primary fiction lead

### The Ash in Transit / Harbinger Protocol — Simon J. Phillips
- **Essay (free):** https://authorsimonphillips.com/2026/05/21/the-ash-in-transit-sci-fi-horror-alien-biomass/
- **Tone match:** Ageing industrial hauler, metallic air, tired machinery, salvage-as-sin.
- **Motifs to adapt (not copy):**
  - Contamination enters as cargo / salvage
  - Heat and life-support feed the threat
  - Crew illness becomes an engineering problem
  - Schedule and quarantine cost delay the truth
  - Voice-like sounds in ducts that may not be human
- **Emotional fit:** Lonely witness, institutional failure, ship that stops belonging to people — aligns with sad/lonely/fear pillars.

> Note: Full novella appears commercial (Kindle). Do not paste copyrighted prose into repo — keep motif notes and links only. See wishlist if a paid read is desired later.

## Secondary fiction lead

### Draconis Prime — Bill Kandiliotis (short)
- **URL:** https://kandiliotis.com/draconis-prime/
- **Motifs:** Hive mind that hates being severed from the main hive; space travel splits the mind; paranoia about which bodies are still “us.”
- **Adapt carefully:** Hive ship tracking our factory ship; range/connection anxiety for the alien side as well as the human side.

## Environmental storytelling prompts (original, for our ship)

Use as prop/log seeds — not imported text:

1. A shift schedule still pinned up for a crew that never clocks in again.
2. A belt junction labeled in three languages; the third scratched out.
3. Processor heat logs trending “within tolerance” while deck sensors disagree.
4. A personal locker with a half-eaten ration and a child’s drawing of a star.
5. Hive residue first appears as “dust” in the filter change log.
