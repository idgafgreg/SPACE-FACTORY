Soft steal: recovery valleys that are *prep and keep-the-lights-on labor* before a forecastable surge (pairs Darkwood day-prep / STALKER take-shelter), with factory mass/throughput as the cost of escape or seal. Refuse clone-comedy workforce, open-world adventure primary, and Frostpunk city-builder replace.
2. **Cheerful company branding is the workplace trap.** *Mouthwashing* deepen (Little White Lies / The Communist / TheGamer / Polle wiki): Pony Express posters and motion-sensor Polle statue preach harmony while docking pay, capping sleep, and treating the last human freighter crew as expendable; cargo hold reveals worthless mouthwash; mascot voice is stitched ad-clip uncanny. Soft steal: cheerful debt-PA / Polle-energy posters that stay bright while sticky grows (pairs 07-25 cheerful debt-PA); empty-protocol optimism as dread, not comedy. Refuse interpersonal murder-mystery primary and freighter-psychosis replace of hive body-horror (07-21 mood already mined).
3. **Every room is a document of absent labor.** *Shadows of Doubt* (PC Gamer / Steam / Eurogamer / ColePowered): hyper-industrialized noir city where apartments and workplaces hold receipts, emails, CCTV, and fingerprints; investigation board links physical scraps; diegetic rain/tannoy/camera whir carry vibe when music drops out. Soft steal light: sticky-bay archaeology as *lived work residue* (time cards, half-filled logs, locker contents) readable in FP without quest-chrome (pairs Tacoma empty workplace / Lacuna sheets light — objects first, not detective loop). Refuse open-world PI primary and neon cyberpunk comedy.
4. **Cleanse the node to put the building back in order.** *Control* deepen (SUPERJUMP / Game Developer / Polygon Macdonald / GamesRadar AWE): Control Points are workplace ritual — clear Hiss corruption and Brutalist geometry snaps back from shifted chaos; mundanity (elevators, labels, offices) collides with the unexplainable; shuttered sectors go dark until power/light labor restores them. Soft steal: sticky footholds that *warp corridor readability* until scrubbed/sealed — architecture as the infection thermometer (pairs Shelter impregnation / We Went Back overnight rewrite). Refuse third-person ability shooter and paranatural lore paste; Brutalism-adjacent already 07-22.
5. **Fiction: the hive is a manufacturing defect.** Motif from *Heart of Thunder* (Raahem Alvi, Clarkesworld Aug 2025): corporate neural/military augmentations fail inside labor-colony workers; parasites gain sentience through a production fault and crawl as an industrial hive mind across extractive grids. Soft steal: biomass that begins as *bad product / failed implant in the plant*, not only an external boarding party (pairs Irene HVAC choice / Factorio pollution debt).
