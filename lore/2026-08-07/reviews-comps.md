## Voices of the Void (mrdrnose) — lonely signal job

- **Itch:** https://mrdrnose.itch.io/votv
- **RPS:** https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/press-many-buttons-to-operate-a-radio-telescope-array-in-this-eerie-indie-games-demo
- **Why it fits:** Closest recent match for *lonely industrial labor that is the game* before horror arrives. Maintenance of array/servers/generator is not a side activity — it *is* the shift. Anticipation systems (radar, alarm reset) teach dread without a chase.
- **Vs prior comps:** Complements Outer Wilds distant-signal loneliness and Firewatch radio intimacy without walking-sim; complements Amnesia Bunker generator clock without WW1 bunker FPS; distinct from Duskers CLI/drone mediation.
- **Anti-drift:** Do not become a signal-scanner minigame or forest explorer. Factory layout stays primary; VotV teaches *tool grammar and pacing*, not a new loop.
